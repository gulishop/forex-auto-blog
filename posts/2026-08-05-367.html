Leverage Trading Kya Hai? Safe Tareeqay Se Use Karne Ke 3 Golden Rules ⚖️

📅 05-08-2026

💡 Imagine karo aapke wallet mein sirf $100 hain, lekin aap $10,000 ki position open karna chahte ho. Yeh magic karta hai **Leverage**! Simple words mein, leverage broker ka diya hua ek tool hai jo aapke chote capital ki buying power ko multiply kar deta hai. Jab aap 1:100 leverage use karte ho, toh aapka $1 market mein $100 ke barabar kaam karta hai, jisse chote account se bhi badi trades li ja sakti hain.

⚠️ Lekin ruko! Trading community mein leverage ko **Double-Edged Sword** (dohari talwar) kaha jata hai. Jitna bada leverage, utna zyada profit ka chance—lekin utna hi tezi se account wash hone ka risk! Naye traders aksar greed mein aakar 1:500 ya 1:1000 jaisa high leverage select kar lete hain. Result? Market ki thodi si opposite movement unka poora account zero kar deti hai.

🎯 Risk ko control mein rakh kar trading karne ke liye yeh 3 golden rules follow karein:

• **Low Leverage Choose Karein:** Beginners ke liye 1:10 ya maximum 1:20 leverage hi best aur safe hai.

• **Strict Stop Loss:** Volatile Forex aur Crypto market mein bina Stop Loss ke trade lagana sabse badi galti hai.

• **Smart Risk Rule:** Kisi bhi single trade mein apne total capital ka 1% se 2% se zyada risk par mat lagayein.

📊 Hamesha yaad rakhein, trading market mein koi "Sure Profit" ya shortcuts nahi hote. Yahan sirf wahi traders long-term profit banate hain jo strict discipline aur proper risk management follow karte hain.
