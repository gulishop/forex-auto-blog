🚀 Leverage Kya Hai Aur Ise Safely Kaise Use Karein?

📅 25-07-2026

📊 Trading ki duniya mein leverage ek aisa powerful tool hai jo aapko kam capital ke sath badi trade size open karne ki permission deta hai. Simple words mein kahein to, ye aapke broker se mila ek temporary loan hota hai. Example ke taur par, agar aapke account mein $100 hain aur aap 1:10 leverage use karte hain, to aap $1,000 worth ki trade control kar sakte hain. Margin kam lagta hai aur profits zyada dikhte hain, lekin story yahan khatam nahi hoti!

⚠️ High leverage ko trading ka "Double-Edged Sword" kaha jata hai. Ye jitni jaldi aapke profits ko multiply kar sakta hai, utni hi tezi se aapka poora account wash bhi kar sakta hai. Naye traders aksar 1:100 ya 1:500 jaise high leverage ki lalach mein aakar big positions le lete hain aur ek choti si market movement par apna saara capital lose kar baithte hain.

💡 Leverage ko safe tarike se handle karne ke liye in basic rules ko hamesha yaad rakhein:

• **Low Leverage Se Start Karein:** Beginners ke liye 1:5 ya maximum 1:10 leverage safe mana jata hai.

• **Stop Loss Is Non-Negotiable:** Trade open karte hi Stop Loss zaroor lagayein taake market aapke against jaye to loss limited rahe.

• **Proper Risk Management:** Ek single trade mein apne total account balance ka 1% ya 2% se zyada risk kabhi mat lein.

📈 Profit har kisi ko chahiye, lekin trading mein long-term tikne ke liye capital protection sabse pehle aata hai. Controlled leverage, risk management aur proper strategy ke sath market ko analyze karein. Yaad rakhein, market mein koi bhi strategy 100% guaranteed nahi hoti, isliye hamesha disciplined aur smart trader banein.
