Top 4 Candlestick Patterns Jo Har Trader Ko Pata Hone Chahiye (Market Ka Trend Samjhne Ke Liye) 🕯️

📅 29-07-2026

💡 Trading charts par price ki movement read karna mushkil lagta hai? Candlestick patterns aapke sabse bade secret weapon hain! Forex aur Crypto trading mein ye patterns aapko chart par buyers aur sellers ki real-time psychology samjhate hain. Aayein dekhte hain wo key patterns jo har beginner trader ko zaroor pata hone chahiye.

🎯 **Hammer & Shooting Star (Reversal Signals):** Jab market downtrend mein ho aur bottom par choti body aur lambi lower wick wali candle (Hammer) bane, toh ye bullish reversal ka signal ho sakta hai. Wahi agar uptrend ke top par Shooting Star dikhe, toh samajh jayein ki sellers pressure bana rahe hain aur trend down ho sakta hai.

🚀 **Engulfing Patterns (Strong Momentum):** Bullish ya Bearish Engulfing pattern tab banta hai jab ek nayi badi candle pichli poori candle ko "engulf" (cover) kar leti hai. Ye market mein strong volume ka ishara hai. Bullish engulfing up-move dikhata hai, jabki bearish engulfing sharp fall ka warning signal hota hai.

⚠️ **Doji Candle (Market Indecision):** Jab candle ki opening aur closing price almost same ho, toh Doji banta hai. Iska matlab hai ki buyers aur sellers ke beech fight chal rahi hai aur market undecided hai. Doji dekh kar kabhi jaldbaazi mein trade mat lein, hamesha agli confirmation candle ka wait karein.

📊 **Pro Tip & Risk Disclaimer:** Trading mein koi bhi pattern 100% "sure profit" ki guarantee nahi deta. Candlesticks ko hamesha Support/Resistance zones aur proper Risk Management (Stop Loss) ke sath use karein taaki aapka capital safe rahe.
