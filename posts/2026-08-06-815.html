🎯 Support aur Resistance Identify Karne Ke 3 Easy Steps: Sahi Entry Aur Exit Kaise Dhoondhein?

📅 06-08-2026

📊 Trading chart par Support aur Resistance bilkul floor aur ceiling ki tarah kaam karte hain. Support wo price level hota hai jahan buyers active ho jate hain aur price ko mazeed neeche girne se rokte hain. Dusri taraf, Resistance wo area hota hai jahan sellers market mein heavy volume ke sath enter hote hain aur price ko upar jaane se rokte hain. In do levels ko sahi samajhna Forex aur Crypto trading ka pehla fundamental step hai.

💡 Strong Support aur Resistance dhoondne ke liye hamesha "Multi-Touch Rule" follow karein. Subse pehle 1-Hour ya 4-Hour chart open karein aur dekhein ke price kis point se kam se kam 2 ya 3 baar bounce back hui hai. Ek baat hamesha yaad rakhein—Support ya Resistance koi ek single exact price line nahi hoti, balki ye ek "Zone" ya range hoti hai jahan price react karti hai.

🎯 Breakout aur Retest par khas nazar rakhein. Jab market kisi strong Resistance ko tod kar upar nikal jati hai, toh wo purani Resistance naya Support ban jati hai (aur vice versa). Hamesha confirmation ka wait karein, jaise koi strong candlestick pattern ya high volume, taake aap fake breakouts aur traps se bach sakein.
