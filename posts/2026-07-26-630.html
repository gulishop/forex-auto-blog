Chart Ka Blueprint: Support Aur Resistance Kaise Identify Karein? 🎯

📅 26-07-2026

📊 Hey Traders! Agar aap Forex ya Crypto market mein chart reading master karna chahte hain, toh Support aur Resistance samajhna aapka pehla step hona chahiye. Simply put, **Support** wo floor hai jahan se buyers active hote hain aur price upar bounce karti hai. **Resistance** wo ceiling hai jahan sellers dominate karte hain aur price drop hoti hai. In levels ko samajhna matlab market ka hidden map crack karna!

💡 Isko charts par spot karne ka tarika bohot simple hai! Sabse pehle Higher Timeframes (jaise 1H, 4H ya Daily chart) open karein. Phir dekhein ki price ne kis price level par kam se kam 2-3 baar touch karke rejection liya hai. Jitne ziada touches honge, wo zone utna hi strong hoga. Pro tip: Single line ki jagah hamesha **Zones (Rectangles)** draw karein kyunki market exact pin-point level ko support nahi karti.

📈 Ek aur smart technique hai Moving Averages (jaise 50 EMA ya 200 EMA) ko use karna, jo **Dynamic Support aur Resistance** ka kaam karte hain. Jab horizontal zone aur EMA overlap hote hain, toh wo ek high-probability trade setup ban sakta hai. Hamesha candlestick patterns aur volume confirmation ke baad hi entry karein.

⚠️ Trading mein koi bhi tool ya indicator 100% guaranteed profit nahi deta. Market unpredictable hoti hai, isliye hamesha proper Risk Management aur Stop Loss zaroor use karein. Ek smart trader profit chasing se ziada apna capital protect karne par focus karta hai.
