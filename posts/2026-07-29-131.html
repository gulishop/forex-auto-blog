Fundamental vs Technical Analysis: Trading Mein Konsa Method Best Hai Aur Dono Ko Kaise Mix Karein? 📊

📅 29-07-2026

📰 Fundamental Analysis (FA) ka matlab hai market ke 'WHY' ko samajhna. Simple words mein, jab aap kisi currency pair ya crypto project ki real value check karte ho—jaise Interest Rates, Inflation data, NFP news, ya project ki real-world utility—toh wo Fundamental Analysis hai. Ye aapko batata hai ke market long-term mein upar jayegi ya neeche, yani asset ka overall health kaisa hai.

📈 Dusri taraf, Technical Analysis (TA) market ke 'WHEN' par focus karta hai. Yahan news se zyada focus price charts, candlestick patterns, support/resistance levels aur indicators (jaise RSI aur Moving Averages) par hota hai. TA aapko batata hai ke exact kis price par entry leni hai aur kahan exit hona hai. Day traders aur scalpers ke liye TA unka sabse bada hathyar hai.

💡 Ab sabse bada sawal: Dono mein se konsa method behtar hai? Truth ye hai ke Pro Traders kabhi bhi sirf ek tarike par depend nahi karte! Fundamental Analysis aapko market ka overall direction aur bias batata hai, jabke Technical Analysis aapko perfect timing aur risk-to-reward ratio deta hai. News dekh kar direction samjhein aur chart dekh kar trade execute karein—ye hybrid formula aapki trading accuracy ko agle level par le ja sakta hai.

⚠️ Ek baat hamesha yaad rakhein: Forex ya Crypto market mein koi bhi analysis 100% guarantee ya sure profit nahi deta. Market hamesha volatile hoti hai, isliye bina proper risk management, position sizing aur Stop Loss ke trade karna risky ho sakta hai.
