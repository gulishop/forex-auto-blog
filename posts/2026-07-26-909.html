4 Trading Mistakes Jo Har Beginner Karta Hai (Aur Inse Kaise Bachein) ⚠️

📅 26-07-2026

Trading ki duniya exciting zaroor hai, lekin shuruat mein aksar naye traders aisi galtiyan kar baithte hain jo unka hard-earned capital waste kar deti hain. Agar aap Forex ya Crypto mein trading start kar rahe hain, toh in common mistakes se bachna aapke liye bohot zaroori hai:

⚠️ **Bina Stop Loss Ke Trade Lagana**: Ye sab se badi galti hai jo beginners karte hain. Emotion mein aakar Stop Loss na lagana aur ye sochna ke "market wapas aayegi", aapka account wipeout kar sakta hai. Trading mein profit banana baad ki baat hai, pehle apne capital ko protect karna seekhein.

📈 **Overtrading Aur Revenge Trading**: Ek loss hua aur aapne emotional hokar turant doosri trade open kar li taake loss recover ho jaye? Isse Revenge Trading kehte hain. Market se kabhi badla lene ki koshish mat karein. Apne daily trades ka limit set karein aur hamesha apne plan par stick rahein.

🚀 **FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) Ke Chakkar Mein Entry**: Green candles ko pump hote dekh kar lagta hai ke train chhoot rahi hai, aur aap top par buy kar lete ho. Hype aur emotion par li gayi entries aksar loss deti hain. Hamesha proper chart analysis aur dip retest ka wait karke hi entry lein.

💡 **High Leverage Aur Fast Money Ka Lalach**: Shuruat mein fast profit ke chakkar mein 50x ya 100x leverage use karna bohot risky hai. High leverage jitna fast profit deta hai, usse double speed se aapka account zero kar sakta hai. Always start with low leverage aur proper risk-to-reward ratio.

🎯 **Trading Ek Skill Hai, Fast Money Scheme Nahi!**: Remember, market mein loss aur profit dono trading ka part hain, koi bhi 100% win-rate guarantee nahi de sakta.
