Crypto Volatility Se Darna Kyun Band Karein? Ye 3 Practical Rules Apnao Aur Smartly Trade Karo 📈⚡

📅 26-07-2026

🌊 Crypto market mein ek hi din mein 10% se 20% ka pump ya dump hona bilkul normal baat hai. Price ke is fast movement ko hi hum **Volatility** kehte hain. Naye traders aksar is speed se ghabra kar wrong decisions le lete hain, lekin ek pro trader ke liye volatility hi profit banane ka asli mauka hoti hai. Bas zaroorat hai isko sahi tarah handle karne ki!

🛡️ **Rule 1: Proper Risk Management Aur Stop Loss Use Karein:** Volatile market mein enter hone se pehle apna risk predefined rakhein. Kabhi bhi apne total capital ka 1-2% se zyada ek trade mein risk mat karein. Strict Stop Loss (SL) lagana zaroori hai taaki sudden market crash aapka poora account wash na kar sake.

📰 **Rule 2: Market Sentiment Aur News Par Nazar Rakhein:** Crypto market mein sudden price swings aksar kisi major economic news, Federal Reserve updates, ya high liquidation events ki wajah se aate hain. Jab market highly unpredictable ho, toh heavy leverage lene se bachein aur news impact ko samajhne ke baad hi position open karein.

🎯 **Rule 3: FOMO Se Bachein Aur Patience Ke Sath Wait Karein:** Fast moving market mein badi green candles dekh kar pump ko chase mat karein. Fear Of Missing Out (FOMO) mein galat entry lena beginners ki sabse badi ghalti hoti hai. Hamesha apne trading plan par tike rahein aur sahi key support ya resistance retest ka wait karein.
