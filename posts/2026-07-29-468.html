Stop Loss Aur Take Profit Ka Sahi Tarika: Account Safe Rakhne Ke 3 Smart Rules 🎯

📅 29-07-2026

📊 Kya aapka trade hamesha Stop Loss hit hone ke baad wapas aapke direction mein chala jata hai? Ya phir thoda sa profit dekh kar aap panic mein jaldi exit kar lete ho? Relax, ye dikkat har beginner trader face karta hai! Forex aur Crypto trading mein apne emotions ko control karne aur capital ko protect karne ke liye Stop Loss (SL) aur Take Profit (TP) sabse zaroori tools hain.

🛡️ **Stop Loss Sahi Jagah Kaise Lagayein:** Randomly $10 ya 20 pips ka SL lagana band karein. Stop Loss hamesha technical levels par hona chahiye—jaise key Support/Resistance zone ke thoda baahar ya recent Swing High/Low ke piche. Isse market ke chote fakeouts aapka Stop Loss trigger nahi karenge. Ek trade mein apne total capital ka sirf 1% se 2% hi risk par rakhein.

🎯 **1:2 Risk-to-Reward Ratio Follow Karein:** Agar aapka Stop Loss $50 ka hai, toh aapka Take Profit kam se kam $100 hona chahiye. Is R:R ratio ka sabse bada fayda ye hai ke agar aap 10 me se sirf 4 trades bhi jeet-te hain, tab bhi aap overall profit mein rahoge! Greed se bachein aur TP ko hamesha realistic key levels par set karein.

💡 **Trailing Stop Loss Ka Smart Use:** Jab trade aapke direction mein move kare aur 1:1 profit hit ho jaye, toh apna SL move karke Entry Price (Break-Even) par le aayein. Isse aapka trade bilkul risk-free ho jayega. Live trade ke dauran emotions mein aakar SL ko piche mat khiskayein—jo plan entry se pehle banaya hai, us par stick rahein.
