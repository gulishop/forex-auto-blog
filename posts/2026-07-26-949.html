📈 Fundamental vs Technical Analysis: Apke Liye Konsi Strategy Behtar Hai? (Aasan Alfaz Mein Samjhein)

📅 26-07-2026

📊 Forex aur Crypto trading ki duniya mein enter hote hi aapko do sabse popular terms sunne ko milte hain — Fundamental Analysis aur Technical Analysis. Technical Analysis poori tarah se price charts, candlestick patterns aur technical indicators (jaise RSI, MACD, aur Moving Averages) par base hota hai. Ye strategy aapko batati hai ke market ka past behavior kaisa raha hai aur short-term mein price kis taraf move kar sakti hai. Scalpers aur day traders ke liye ye perfect timing samajhne ka sabse acha zariya hai.

💡 Dusri taraf, Fundamental Analysis kisi bhi asset ki real value aur uske piche ki story ko samajhne ka naam hai. Forex trading mein hum economic news events, interest rates, GDP aur inflation data ko track karte hain. Wahi Crypto market mein project ki utility, team strength, tokenomics aur real-world adoption ko dekha jata hai. Simple words mein, Fundamental Analysis aapko batata hai ke koi currency ya coin long-term mein kitna strong hai aur *kyun* buy karna chahiye.

🎯 Toh pro traders konsi strategy use karte hain? Secret ye hai ke wo DONO ko combine karte hain! Fundamental Analysis aapko sahi asset choose karne mein help karta hai (kya buy karna hai), jabki Technical Analysis aapko perfect entry aur exit timing batata hai (kab buy karna hai). Lekin hamesha yaad rakhein ke market unpredictable hoti hai aur koi bhi strategy 100% sure profit ki guarantee nahi deti, isliye strict Risk Management aur Stop Loss hamesha follow karein.
