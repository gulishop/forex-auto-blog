Crypto Volatility Se Darna Kyo Chhodna Chahiye? 3 Rules Jo Har Trader Ko Pata Hone Chahiye 📈

📅 07-08-2026

📊 Crypto market ka naam sunte hi sabse pehle ek hi word dimaag mein aata hai—*Volatility*! Ek din mein price 10% up, aur agle hi ghante 15% down. Naye traders is roller-coaster ride se dar kar panic selling kar baithte hain. Lekin sach ye hai ke volatility hi wo cheez hai jo crypto trading mein real opportunities create karti hai. Isse darna nahi, samajhna seekho!

⚠️ Volatile market mein survive karne ka pehla golden rule hai strict *Risk Management*. Bina Stop Loss (SL) ke trade mein enter hona matlab bina brakes ke car chalana. Always remember: kabhi bhi apni total capital ka 1% se 2% se zyada ek single trade mein risk mat karein. Agar market fast move bhi kare, toh aapka account blown-out hone se bach jayega.

🎯 Volatility ko measure karne ke liye tools ka sahara lein. ATR (Average True Range) aur Bollinger Bands jaise technical indicators aapko batate hain ke price me kitni movement expected hai. Saath hi, Crypto Fear & Greed Index check karein. Jab market mein extreme fear ho, tab akshar best risk-to-reward setups milte hain.

💡 High volatility ke dauran hamesha leverage kam rakhein aur position size chota karein. FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) mein aakar impulsive trades lene ke bajaye sahi level ka wait karein. Remember, market hamesha yahin hai, patience hi aapka sabse bada edge hai.
