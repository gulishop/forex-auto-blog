Demo Se Real Account Tak Ka Safar: Safe Smart Trading Strategy 🚀

📅 26-07-2026

📊 Demo account par profit banana aasan lagta hai kyunki wahan virtual money aur zero risk hota hai. Lekin jaise hi aap Real Account par shift hote ho, Fear aur Greed jaise real emotions enter ho jaate hain. Successful trader banne ke liye sabse pehle aapko demo ki fearless practice aur real account ke emotional discipline ka sahi balance banana padega.

💡 Real account mein jump karte hi bada capital lagane ki galti bilkul mat karo. Hamesha chote deposit aur micro-lots se start karo taaki emotional pressure kam ho. Ek golden rule yaad rakho: kisi bhi single trade par apne total capital ka 1% se 2% se zyada risk mat lo. Stop-Loss lagana aapka sabse bada savior hai!

⚠️ Demo account par jo strategy test ki thi, us par stick raho. Har trade ko track karne ke liye ek Trading Journal banao jahan entry, exit aur trading reason note ho. Remember, market unpredictable hoti hai aur trading mein koi "sure profit" guarantee ya magic formula nahi hota. Losses bhi business ka part hain, bas unhe chota rakhna seekhna hai.
