Trading Psychology: Fear Aur Greed Ko Control Karke Profits Secure Kaise Karein? 🧠📈

📅 29-07-2026

⚠️ Trading mein 90% naye traders ke account wash hone ki sabse badi waja indicators ka galat hona nahi, balki unke apne emotions hote hain. Market mein Fear (darr) aur Greed (laalach) aapke sabse bade dushman hain. Jab price tezi se upar jata hai toh FOMO mein aakar top pe buy kar lena, ya loss hone par "Revenge Trading" karke market se badla lena — ye wo galtiyan hain jo ache se ache trading setup ko bhi barbaad kar deti hain.

💡 **Disciplined Trading Plan Follow Karein:** Har trade mein enter hone se pehle apna Stop Loss (SL) aur Take Profit (TP) pehle se set karein. Jab aapka risk pre-defined hoga, toh market ke har tick pe aap panic nahi honge. Apne total capital ka sirf 1% se 2% hi ek trade mein risk pe lagayein.

🎯 **Trading Journal Maintain Karein:** Ek diary ya digital sheet banayein jahan har trade ki waja likhein. Kya aapne setup dekh kar entry li ya emotion mein aakar? Journaling aapko impulsive trading se bachati hai aur aapki weaknesses ko spot karti hai.

🧘‍♂️ **Break Lena Seekhein:** Ek do stop loss hit hone ke baad screen ko band kar dein. Real trader wo nahi jo har waqt trade kare, balki wo hai jo sahi mauke ka wait kare. Remember, market hamesha yahin rahegi, aapka capital nahi agar aapne discipline chhoda!

📊 Remember: Forex aur Crypto trading mein koi 100% guarantee ya shortcut nahi hota. Consistent profits sirf strict risk management aur emotional control se hi milte hain.
