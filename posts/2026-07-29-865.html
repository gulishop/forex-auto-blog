Support Aur Resistance Identify Karne Ka Secret: Ye 3 Simple Steps Try Karein! 🎯

📅 29-07-2026

📊 Trading chart ko dekh kar confuse ho jate ho ke market kahan se reverse hogi? Support aur Resistance (S/R) trading ke do sab se important pillars hain. Support wo "floor" hai jahan price girna ruk jati hai, aur Resistance wo "ceiling" hai jahan price upar jana ruk jati hai. Agar aapne inko sahi se spot kar liya, toh aapki trading accuracy next level par ja sakti hai!

💡 **Swing Highs Aur Lows Dhoondhein:** Support aur Resistance identify karne ka sab se easy tareeka hai chart par major "Swing Highs" aur "Swing Lows" ko mark karna. Past mein price jis price level se kam se kam 2-3 baar bounce back hui hai, wo ek strong zone banta hai. Sahi tareeka ye hai ke aap single line ke bajaye ek **Zone/Box** draw karein, kyunki market exact price hamesha follow nahi karti.

📈 **Timeframe Aur Volume Check Karein:** Higher timeframes (jaise 4H ya Daily charts) ke levels ziada strong aur reliable hote hain. Jab price kisi strong Resistance ko heavy volume ke sath break karti hai, toh wo baad mein Support ban jata hai (jisey "Role Reversal" kehte hain). Aise breakout trades beginners ke liye kafi helpful hote hain.

⚠️ **Fakeouts Se Bachein (Pro Tip):** Level touch hote hi blindly trade mat pakdo! Direct entry lene ke bajaye hamesha price action confirmation ya retest ka wait karein. Trading market unpredictable hoti hai, is liye stop-loss lagana kabhi mat bhoolein. Koi bhi strategy 100% profit ki guarantee nahi deti, hamesha apna risk proper manage karein.
