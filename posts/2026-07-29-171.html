Forex Mein Account Wash Hone Se Kaise Bachayein? Try Karein Ye 3 Essential Risk Management Rules 🛡️

📅 29-07-2026

Kya aapka Forex account baar baar wash ho jata hai? Trading mein profit kamana zaroori hai, lekin apne capital ko protect karna usse bhi zyada zaroori hai. Sahi risk management ke bina duniya ki sabse acchi strategy bhi fail ho sakti hai. Aaiye dekhte hain wo practical tips jo aapko ek disciplined trader bana sakti hain:

📊 **1% Se 2% Rule Always Follow Karein**: Trading ka sabse pehla usool hai capital preservation. Kisi bhi single trade mein apne total account balance ka 1% se 2% se zyada risk kabhi mat lein. Agar aapka balance $1,000 hai, toh ek trade par maximum risk $10-$20 hona chahiye. Is rule se consecutive losses ke bawajood aapka account safe rahega.

🎯 **Stop Loss (SL) Ke Bina Trade Mat Karein**: Forex market bohot fast aur unpredictable hoti hai, khas kar high-impact news ke waqt. Stop Loss aapka safety helmet hai. Chart par support/resistance levels ko dekhte huye trade lene se pehle hi exit point decide karein. Trade chalne ke baad emotional ho kar SL ko peeche hatana sabse badi galti hai.

💡 **1:2 Risk-to-Reward Ratio Set Karein**: Hamesha aisa trade setup dhoondhein jahan Risk-to-Reward Ratio kam se kam 1:2 ho. Iska matlab agar aap $10 ka risk le rahe hain, toh aapka potential profit target minimum $20 hona chahiye. Is strategy ki wajah se agar aapki win rate sirf 40-50% bhi ho, tab bhi aap long run mein profitable rahenge.

⚠️ **Lot Size Aur Over-Leveraging Se Bachein**: Account balance ke hisaab se sahi lot size choose karein. Loss hone par gusse mein aakar "revenge trading" mat karein aur na hi over-leveraging karein. Remember, consistent profits discipline se aate hain, overnight greed se nahi.
