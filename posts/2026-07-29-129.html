Leverage Se Profit Hoga Ya Account Wash? Jaaniye Safe Trading Ke 3 Golden Rules 🎯

📅 29-07-2026

💡 Leverage Forex aur Crypto market ka sabse attractive tool hai, lekin yeh ek double-edged sword (dohri talwar) ki tarah kaam karta hai. Simply bolun toh, leverage aapko chote capital ke saath badi position size lene ki power deta hai. For example, 1:100 leverage ke saath aap sirf $100 se $10,000 ki trade control kar sakte hain. Sunne mein amazing lagta hai, right? Lekin jitna zyaada leverage, utna hi zyaada risk!

⚠️ Naye traders aksar quick profit ke chakkar mein 50x ya 100x high leverage use karte hain. Natija? Jab market thoda sa bhi against jata hai, unka account wash (liquidate) ho jata hai. Leverage aapka profit zarur multiply karta hai, lekin loss ko bhi utni hi speed se bada karta hai. Isliye ise bina risk management ke use karna direct gambling hai.

📊 Agar aap leverage ko safely use karke market mein long-term tikna chahte hain, toh ye rules follow karein:

• Low Leverage Choose Karein: Beginners ke liye 1:5 se 1:10 tak ka leverage safe zone hai. High leverage se door rahein.

• Always Use Stop-Loss: Leverage trade mein bina Stop Loss ke enter hona sabse badi galti hai. Yeh aapke account ka safety net hai.

• Strict Position Sizing: Ek single trade mein apne total capital ka sirf 1% se 2% hi risk par rakhein.

🚀 Trading ek marathon hai, koi 100-meter sprint nahi. Overnight rich banne ke bajaye risk control karna seekhein, profit khud-ba-khud follow karega. Market mein koi guaranteed profit nahi hota, isliye hamesha smart aur disciplined trader banein.
