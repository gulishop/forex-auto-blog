Account Wash Hone Se Kaise Bachayein? Stop Loss Aur Take Profit Set Karne Ke 3 Rules 🎯

📅 29-07-2026

📊 Forex aur Crypto trading mein bina Stop Loss (SL) aur Take Profit (TP) ke enter hona bilkul waisa hai jaise bina seatbelt aur brakes ke fast car chalana! Aksar naye traders emotion mein aakar trades open chhod dete hain, aur jab market sudden move leti hai toh account zero ho jata hai. Smart traders chart par enter hone se pehle hi apna maximum risk aur profit target fix kar lete hain.

💡 **Stop Loss Ko Technical Levels Par Set Karein:** SL ko kabhi bhi kisi random dollar amount (jaise "bas $5 ka loss") par mat rakhein. Hamesha key Support, Resistance ya Market Structure ke thoda bahar SL lagayein. Is tarah aapka trade market ki normal fluctuation (noise) se bacha rahega aur unnecessary hit nahi hoga.

🎯 **Minimum 1:2 Risk-to-Reward Ratio Maintain Karein:** Take Profit (TP) set karte waqt hamesha realistic targets rakhein. Agar aap ek trade mein $10 ka risk le rahe hain (SL), toh aapka profit target kam se kam $20 (TP) hona chahiye. Is strategy se agar aapki 50% trades galat bhi ho jayein, tab bhi aap month end par overall green (profit) mein rahenge.

⚠️ **"Set and Forget" Rule Follow Karein:** Trade active hone ke baad apne SL ko fear mein aakar peeche mat khainchein aur na hi greed mein TP ko bina waja badlein. Trading mein koi financial guarantee ya sure profit nahi hota, isliye disciplined rehna zaroori hai. Chote losses ko accept karna seekhein taake aapka capital hamesha safe rahe.
