⚠️ 4 Trading Mistakes Jo Naye Traders Ko Dooba Deti Hain (Aur Unse Kaise Bachein)

📅 26-07-2026

📊 **High Leverage Ka Jaal:** Naye traders trading start karte hi 50x ya 100x leverage use karne lagte hain. Quick profit ke chakkar mein risk management ko bhool jana sabse bari galti hai. High leverage aapke account ko seconds mein zero kar sakta hai. Always start with low leverage (1:5 ya 1:10) aur ek trade mein sirf 1-2% capital risk karein.

🛑 **No Stop Loss, Big Loss:** "Market abhi wapas aayegi" wali mentality trader ki sabse bari dushman hai. Bina Stop Loss ke trade lagana aisa hai jaise bina brakes ke gaadi chalana. Market aapke against ja sakti hai, isliye har trade open karne se pehle apna maximum loss limit (Stop Loss) zaroor fix karein.

💡 **FOMO Aur Revenge Trading:** Jab koi Crypto ya Forex pair sudden pump karta hai, toh naye traders emotional ho kar top par buy kar lete hain (FOMO). Aur jab loss hota hai, toh usse turant recover karne ke liye over-trading karte hain (Revenge Trading). Remember, market mein patience aur discipline hi aapka sabse bada tool hai.

🎯 **Bina Proper Plan Ke Trading:** Trading ek professional business hai, koi gambling nahi. Agar aapke paas clear entry, exit points aur minimum 1:2 Risk-to-Reward ratio ka plan nahi hai, toh long-term profit banana mushkil hai. Always trade your plan and maintain a trading journal.
