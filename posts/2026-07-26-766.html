Forex Mein Account Wash Hone Se Kaise Bachayein? Risk Management Ke 4 Golden Rules 🛡️

📅 26-07-2026

📉 Forex trading mein successful hone ka asli secret koi magical indicator nahi, balki solid Risk Management hai! Agar aapka risk control strong hai, toh 50% win rate ke sath bhi aap long-term mein profitable reh sakte hain. Lekin bina risk management ke trading karna bina brakes ke car chalane jaisa hai.

🎯 **1% Rule Follow Karein:** Har single trade par apne total equity ka sirf 1% se 2% risk karein. Agar aapka account $1,000 ka hai, toh aapka max loss per trade $10-$20 se zyada nahi hona chahiye. Is rule se consecutive losses ke baad bhi aapka account kabhi wash nahi hoga.

🛑 **Stop Loss (SL) Par Zero Compromise:** Market unpredictable hoti hai aur high-impact news ke waqt bade spikes aate hain. Trade open karne se pehle apna Stop Loss zaroor set karein. Emotions mein aakar SL ko remove ya stretch bilkul na karein—yehi ek discipline trader kipehchan hai.

⚖️ **Proper Risk-to-Reward Ratio (1:2):** Hamesha aisi trades execute karein jahan aapka potential profit aapke risk se kam se kam dugna ho. Agar aap $10 risk kar rahe hain, toh target kam se kam $20 hona chahiye. Correct position sizing aur lot size se hi ye balance maintain hota hai.

⚠️ **Revenge Trading Se Door Rahein:** Continuous loss hone par gusse mein aakar badi lot size lagana sabse bada trap hai. Daily loss limit decide karein—jab limit hit ho jaye, screen off karein aur next day fresh mind ke sath aayein. Remember, risk management profit ki guarantee nahi deta, lekin aapko market mein lambe arse tak survive karne mein madad karta hai.
