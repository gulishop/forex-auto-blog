Support Aur Resistance Sahi Tarike Se Kaise Spot Karein? 3 Simple Rules Jo Har Trader Ko Pata Hone Chahiye 🎯

📅 02-08-2026

📊 Trading charts ko samajhna pehli baar mein complex lagta hai? Par tension mat lein! Support aur Resistance aapke chart ke "Floor" aur "Ceiling" ki tarah hote hain. Support wo price zone hai jahan buyers active hote hain aur price ko neeche girne se rokte hain. Dusri taraf, Resistance wo area hai jahan sellers dominant hote hain aur price wahan se reject hoti hai. In 2 levels ko samajhna trading ki buniyad hai.

💡 Isko identify karne ke liye hamesha multi-touch points dhoondhein. Chart par kam se kam 2 se 3 aise swing highs ya lows dekhein jahan se price baar baar reverse hui ho. Ek sabse zaroori tip: Support aur Resistance ko kabhi ek single sharp line mat samjhein, ye hamesha ek "Zone" ya price area hota hai. Noise se bachne ke liye aap Line Chart ka use karke clear levels spot kar sakte hain.

⚠️ Hamesha yaad rakhein, trading market mein koi bhi strategy 100% guaranteed nahi hoti! Breakout dikhte hi bina soche samjhe enter mat hoyein, kyunki market mein fakeouts (false breakouts) bohot common hain. Hamesha confirmation candle ka wait karein. Apne capital ko safe rakhne ke liye proper Risk Management aur Stop Loss ka istemal har trade mein lazmi karein.
