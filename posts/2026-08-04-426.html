Forex Mein Account Blow Hone Se Kaise Bachayein? Ye 3 Risk Management Rules Seekho 🛡️

📅 04-08-2026

🛡️ **1% Rule Follow Karo**: Trading mein sabse badi galti hoti hai over-risking. Ek single trade mein apne total account balance ka sirf 1% se 2% hi risk par lagayein. Agar aapka account $1,000 ka hai, toh per trade max $10-$20 ka risk lein. Is simple rule se 10 consecutive loss ke baad bhi aapka account safe rahega aur aap emotional control lose nahi karenge.

🎯 **Stop Loss Ko Apna Best Friend Banayein**: Bina Stop Loss (SL) ke trade karna bina seatbelt gadi chalane jaisa hai. Trade open karne se pehle hi apna exit plan fix rakho. Market mein enter hone se pehle sahi support ya resistance par SL set karein. Hamesha yaad rakhein—apne SL ko loss mein aakar peeche shift mat karein, greed aur hope trading se bachein!

📊 **1:2 Risk-to-Reward Ratio Maintain Karein**: Successful traders ka secret inki high win-rate nahi, balki unka Risk-to-Reward Ratio (RRR) hota hai. Hamesha aisi trades dhoondhein jahan agar aap $10 risk kar rahe hain, toh kam se kam $20 profit ka target ho. Is math ki wajah se agar aapki 50% trades galat bhi ho jayein, tab bhi aap end of the month overall profit mein rahoge.

⚠️ **Position Sizing Aur Leverage Control**: High leverage dekh kar bada lot size lena naye traders ki sabse badi galti hai. Leverage ek double-edged sword hai. Apne account balance ke hisab se exact lot size calculate karein taaki market ke ek chote se fluctuations se aapka account wipe out na ho jaye.

Remember, Forex market mein koi "sure-shot guarantee" ya overnight wealth nahi hoti—discipline aur proper risk management hi aapka real profit maker hai. Happy Trading!📈
