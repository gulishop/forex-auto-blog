Support Aur Resistance Sahi Tarah Se Spot Kaise Karein? 🎯 Naye Traders Ke Liye 3 Practical Steps

📅 29-07-2026

💡 Forex aur Crypto market mein successful trading ka sabse pehla aur sabse important rule hai key price levels ko samajhna. Support aur Resistance trading chart ke wo unseen "floor" aur "ceiling" hote hain jahan se price baar baar reverse hoti hai. Agar aap in levels ko sahi se draw karna seekh gaye, toh aapke trades ki precision kafi improve ho sakti hai!

📊 **1. Multiple Touchpoints Dhoondhein**: Chart par pichle 2 ya 3 aise spots mark karein jahan se market ne sharp bounce-back kiya ho. Jitni baar price kisi level ko touch karke reverse hoti hai, wo Support ya Resistance utna hi strong mana jata hai. Single touch point ko kabhi bhi strong level mat samjhein.

🎯 **2. Lines Nahi, Zones Draw Karein**: Naye traders sabse badi galti ye karte hain ke wo ek single line draw kar dete hain. Market exact ek price level par react nahi karti, balki ek range mein behave karti hai. Isliye candle ke wicks aur bodies dono ko cover karte hue hamesha ek zone banayein.

📈 **3. Rejection Wicks Aur Round Numbers**: Candlesticks par lambi wicks (shadows) strong price rejection ka signal hoti hain. Iske alawa, round numbers (jaise Gold ka 2000 ya BTC ka $60,000) naturally strong psychological Support/Resistance ka kaam karte hain kyun ke wahan buyers aur sellers ke heavy orders hote hain.

⚠️ **Remember**: Market mein koi bhi strategy 100% sure nahi hoti, fakeouts bhi hote hain! Isliye hamesha proper risk management aur Stop Loss ka use karein. Pehle in rules ko chart par observation ke sath practice karein.
