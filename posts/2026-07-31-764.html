⚠️ Naye Traders Ki 4 Badi Galtiyan (Aur Unse Bachne Ka Sahi Tarika)

📅 31-07-2026

Forex aur Crypto market mein enter hote hi har koi jaldi profit banana chahta hai. Lekin real trading mein bina proper planning ke aana matlab apne capital ko khatre mein daalna. Agar aap bhi apni trading journey shuru kar rahe hain, toh in common mistakes se zaroor bachein:

🚨 **No Risk Management:** Naye traders account banate hi high leverage use karte hain aur FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) mein aakar poora capital ek hi trade mein laga dete hain. Capital ko safe rakhna profit banane se zyada zaroori hai. Hamesha har trade par sirf 1% se 2% ka risk lein.

🛑 **Stop Loss Ignore Karna:** Loss accept na karna beginners ki sabse badi galti hai. Jab market khilaf jata hai, toh "wapas aayega" is umeed mein rehne se account wash ho sakta hai. Trade open karne se pehle apna Stop Loss (SL) aur Take Profit (TP) zaroor set karein.

🤯 **Emotional & Revenge Trading:** Ek trade mein loss hua aur aapne turant loss recover karne ke liye jazbaat mein aakar aur trades open kar di? Isse revenge trading kehte hain. Market se badla lene ki bajaye thoda break lein aur apne trading rules par strict rahein.

📊 **Bina Strategy Ke Trade Lena:** Social media hype ya random Telegram signals par blind trades lena trading nahi, gambling hai. Pehle basic technical analysis seekhein, apna ek simple trading plan banayein aur risk-to-reward ratio samajhein.

💡 Remember, trading koi raton-raat ameer banne ki scheme nahi hai. Isme success sirf discipline aur continuous learning se milti hai.
