Trading Mein Leverage Kya Hai? High Profits Ya Account Wash — Safely Kaise Use Karein 🚀

📅 27-07-2026

📊 Trading mein **Leverage** ek aisa powerful tool hai jo aapki buying power ko kai guna badha deta hai. Simply put, ye broker ki taraf se mila ek temporary boost hota hai. Agar aapke wallet mein $100 hain aur aap 1:100 leverage use karte hain, toh aap market mein $10,000 tak ki trade control kar sakte hain. Chote capital se bada exposure milna exciting lagta hai, lekin yaad rakhein—profits jitne fast hote hain, losses bhi utne hi tez ho sakte hain!

💡 **Low Leverage Se Shuruat Karein:** Naye traders aksar 1:500 ya 1:1000 jaise high leverage dekh kar lalach mein aa jaate hain, jo account wash hone ki sabse badi wajah hai. As a beginner, pehle 1:5 ya maximum 1:10 leverage se start karein. Low leverage aapke capital ko sudden market volatility se safe rakhta hai aur aapko sikhne ka room deta hai.

⚠️ **Stop Loss Ke Bina Trade Mat Karein:** High leverage trading bina **Stop Loss (SL)** ke karna matlab bina brakes ke gaadi chalana hai. Trade open karne se pehle hamesha apna risk calculate karein. Safe trading ka golden rule hai ke kisi bhi single trade par apne total account balance ka 1% se 2% se zyada risk kabhi mat lein.

🎯 **Disciplined Mindset Banayein:** Leverage koi "Get Rich Quick" scheme nahi hai, balki ek trading tool hai. High leverage tabhi work karta hai jab aapke paas strong risk management aur proper strategy ho. Greedy hone ke bajaye slow aur steady growth par focus karein.
