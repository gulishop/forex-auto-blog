Top 4 Trading Mistakes Jo Naye Traders Karte Hain (Aur Inse Kaise Bachein) 🛑

📅 13-08-2026

🚀 Crypto aur Forex market mein enter hote hi har koi quick profits ka sapna dekhta hai, lekin reality ye hai ke 90% naye traders shuruat mein hi apna capital lose kar dete hain. Market volatile zaroor hai, lekin loss ki sabse badi wajah wo common mistakes hain jo easily avoid ki ja sakti hain.

⚠️ **1. Over-Leverage Aur Big Risk:** High leverage dekh kar excitement mein aana sabse dangerous hai. Naye traders 50x ya 100x leverage le kar ek hi trade mein poora account risk par laga dete hain. Pro tip: Always remember, har trade par apne total capital ka sirf 1-2% hi risk par rakhein taake aapka account safe rahe.

🎯 **2. Stop Loss Na Use Karna:** Loss accept na karna aur Stop Loss hatana account wipeout ki sabse badi wajah hai. Market hamesha aapke mutabiq nahi chalegi. Emotionally trade mat karein—trade open karne se pehle hi apna exit plan aur Stop Loss fix karein.

📈 **3. FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) Trading:** Jab koi coin ya Forex pair already huge pump kar chuka ho, tab excitement mein jump karna risk ko double kar deta hai. Bhagti hui train ke piche mat bhagein, hamesha market retracement aur proper confirmation ka wait karein.

💡 **4. Without Strategy Trade Karna:** Bina proper risk-reward ratio aur technical analysis ke trading karna gambling jaisa hai. Social media signals par blindly depend karne ke bajaye ek simple strategy master karein aur apna trading journal maintain rakhein. Trading mein consistency strategy aur discipline se aati hai.
