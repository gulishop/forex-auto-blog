Trading Psychology: 3 Ways Emotions Ko Control Karke Account Wash Hone Se Kaise Bachein? 🧠📉

📅 28-07-2026

⚠️ **FOMO Aur Greed Aapka Sabse Bada Dushman Hai:** Crypto aur Forex trading mein chart analysis se ziada tough hota hai apne mind ko control karna. Jab market sudden pump hoti hai, toh Fear Of Missing Out (FOMO) mein aakar log bina kisi plan ke buy kar lete hain. Result? Top par buy aur heavy loss! Greed, panic selling aur over-trading hi zyadatar traders ke account wash hone ki sabse badi wajah banti hain.

🎯 **Strict Trading Plan Aur Risk Management Follow Karein:** Emotions ko side par rakhne ka sabse best tarika hai Rules-based trading. Har trade lene se pehle apna Entry Point, Stop Loss (SL) aur Take Profit (TP) define karein. Jab aapka Risk Management pehle se set hoga, toh market ki volatility dekh kar aap panic mein galat decisions nahi lenge. Market se badla lene (revenge trading) ke bajaye apne setup par trust karein.

💡 **Losses Ko Game Ka Hissa Samjhein:** World-class traders ka secret koi hidden indicator nahi, balki emotional stability hai. Loss har trader ko hota hai, lekin professional trader loss ke baad chill rehta hai aur beginner frustration mein aakar aur bada loss kar deta hai. Hamesha wahi capital risk karein jise lose karna aap afford kar sakein, taake aapka mind calm rahe aur decisions logical hon.
