Ek Step-by-Step Trading Plan Kaise Banayein? Ye 4 Rules Aapki Trading Badal Denge 🎯

📅 10-08-2026

📊 Bina plan ke trading karna matlab blindfolded hoke car chalana! Trading plan aapka personal roadmap hai jo aapko emotional decisions aur heavy losses se bachata hai. Sabse pehle apna trading style define karein—kya aap Scalper hain, Day Trader hain ya Swing Trader? Apne realistic profit targets aur trading hours fix karein taake aap over-trading se bach sakein.

⚠️ **Risk Management Rules Set Karein:** Har trade par kitna risk lena hai, ye pehle se decided hona chahiye. Smart traders kisi bhi single trade par apni total capital ka 1% se 2% se zyada risk nahi lete. Apne plan mein Stop Loss (SL) aur Risk-to-Reward ratio (kam se kam 1:2) zaroor shamil karein. SL ke bina trade lagana matlab market ko apna paisa gift karna hai!

🎯 **Clear Entry & Exit Strategy:** Market mein kab enter hona hai aur kab profit book karke nikalna hai, iska exact rule banayein. E.g., kya aap Breakout par trade karenge ya Support/Resistance bounce par? Jab aapke strategy ke saare rules match hon, tabhi trade execute karein. FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) mein aakar kabhi random trades mat lein.

💡 **Daily Trading Journal Maintain Karein:** Ek kamyab trader hamesha apni galtiyon se seekhta hai. Daily ek journal banayein jisme aapki trade entry, exit price, reason aur emotions ka record ho. Weekly review karne se aapko samajh aayega ke konsi strategy aapke liye sahi kaam kar rahi hai. Trading consistency aur discipline ka khel hai, yahan koi overnight shortcut ya guaranteed profit nahi hota.
