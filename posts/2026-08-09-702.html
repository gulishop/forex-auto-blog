Leverage Kya Hai Aur Isse Account Wash Hone Se Kaise Bachayein? 3 Practical Tips 📈

📅 09-08-2026

📊 Trading mein **Leverage** ka matlab hai apne broker se temporary capital borrow karke apni buying power ko multiply karna. Aasan lafzon mein, agar aapke account mein sirf $100 hain aur aap 1:100 leverage select karte hain, toh aap $10,000 tak ki market position size control kar sakte hain. Iska sabse bada advantage ye hai ke aap chote account balance se bhi ache market moves ko capture kar sakte hain.

⚠️ Lekin har coin ke do sides hote hain! Trading mein leverage ko ek **Double-Edged Sword** (dohri talwar) mana jata hai. Jitni tezi se ye aapke potential profits ko badhati hai, utni hi tezi se ye aapke losses ko bhi multiply karti hai. Beginner traders aksar greed mein aa kar 1:500 ya 1:1000 jaisi high leverage use kar lete hain, aur market ki thodi si opposite movement hotey hi unka poora account wash (liquidate) ho jata hai.

💡 Leverage ko safely aur professionally handle karne ke 3 golden rules:

• **Low Leverage Choose Karein:** Capital protect karne ke liye beginners hamesha low leverage (1:10 se 1:20 max) se hi start karein.

• **Strict Stop-Loss Use Karein:** Market kabhi bhi unexpected move kar sakti hai, isliye bina Stop-Loss ke koi trade open na karein.

• **1-2% Risk Rule:** Single trade mein apne total account balance ka sirf 1% se 2% risk par rakhein taake aap long-term game mein bane rahein.
