Trading Plan Kaise Banayein? 4 Simple Steps Jo Aapko Big Losses Se Bachayenge 🎯

📅 15-08-2026

💡 Agar aap market mein bina kisi roadmap ke trade le rahe hain, toh sach yeh hai ke aap trading nahi balki gambling kar rahe hain. Ek solid trading plan aapka personal compass hota hai jo aapko emotional decisions aur FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) se bachata hai. Forex ho ya Crypto, consistency sirf discipline se aati hai!

📊 **Step 1: Apna Style aur Market Chunein**

Sabse pehle decide karein ke aap kya trade karenge—Major Forex pairs ya High-volatility Crypto? Phir apna trading style define karein (Scalping, Day Trading ya Swing Trading). Apne entry triggers clear rakhein, jaise Support/Resistance breakout ya RSI divergence, taake trade lete waqt koi confusion na ho.

⚠️ **Step 2: Strict Risk Management Set Karein**

Professional traders profit se pehle apna risk calculate karte hain. Ek golden rule yaad rakhein: Kisi bhi single trade par apni total capital ka 1% se 2% se zyada risk mat lein. Trade lagane se pehle apna Stop Loss (SL) aur Take Profit (TP) fix karein taake unexpected market moves aapka account wipe out na kar sakein.

📈 **Step 3: Trading Journal Maintain Karein**

Har trade ke baad record karein ke aapne trade kyun li, kitna profit/loss hua aur us waqt aapka mindset kaisa tha. Trading journal maintain karne se aapko apni galtiyon ka pata chalta hai aur aap unhe dobara repeat karne se bachte hain.
