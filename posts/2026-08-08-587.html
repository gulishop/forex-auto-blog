4 Badi Trading Galtiyan Jo Naye Traders Ko Loss Karwati Hain 🚨

📅 08-08-2026

Hey traders! Agar aap Forex ya Crypto market mein naye hain, toh ye post aapka hard-earned money bacha sakti hai. Trading mein success ke liye chart reading jitna zaroori hai, utna hi zaroori common mistakes se bachna hai. Aaiye dekhte hain wo konsi 4 main galtiyan hain jo aksar beginners karte hain:

⚠️ **FOMO Mein Aakar Overtrading Karna**: Green candles dekh kar bina kisi setup ke market mein jump karna sabse bada trap hai. Naye traders Fear Of Missing Out (FOMO) ki wajah se impulsive trades lete hain aur heavy loss kar baithte hain. Market hamesha mauka deti hai, isliye patience ke sath apne entry point ka wait karein.

🎯 **Stop Loss Use Na Karna**: Stop Loss aapki trading journey ka seatbelt hai. Bina Stop Loss ke trade lagana ka matlab hai bina brakes ke gaadi chalana! Ek single market dump aapka poora account wash kar sakta hai. Apne capital ko protect karne ke liye per trade sirf 1-2% risk lein.

💡 **Revenge Trading Aur Emotional Decisions**: Loss hona trading ka normal hissa hai, lekin loss hote hi emotional ho kar market se badla (revenge) lene ki koshish mat karein. Revenge trading mein over-leveraging hoti hai jo account ko zero kar deti hai. Trading ko business ki tarah treat karein, casino ki tarah nahi.

📈 **Bina Proper Strategy Ke Jump Karna**: Random Buy aur Sell daba dena trading nahi hoti. Technical analysis, support/resistance aur risk-to-reward ratio samjhe bina trade lagana gambling hai. Live account par paisa lagane se pehle market mechanics ko samajhna behad zaroori hai.
