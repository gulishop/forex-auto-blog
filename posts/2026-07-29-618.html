Ye 4 Galtiyan Aapka Trading Account Zero Kar Sakti Hain 🚨 (Aur Inse Kaise Bachein)

📅 29-07-2026

📈 Forex aur Crypto market mein daily hazaron naye traders aate hain, lekin reality ye hai ke aksar log shuruati dino mein apna capital lose kar dete hain. Reason? Ye market strategy se pehle emotions se chalte hain. Agar aap trading mein long-term successful hona chahte hain, toh in common mistakes se bachein:

⚠️ **Bina Stop Loss Ke Trade Karna**: Beginners ki sabse badi galati hoti hai Stop Loss (SL) na lagana. Log sochte hain "market wapas upar aayega", lekin ek bada crash poora account zero kar deta hai. Hamesha trade lagane se pehle apna exit point fix karein aur per trade 1-2% se zyaada risk na lein.

📊 **Overtrading Aur Revenge Trading**: Ek trade mein loss hua nahi ke naye traders gusse mein aakar turant doosri trade open kar lete hain taake loss recover ho sake. Isko "Revenge Trading" kehte hain. Rule yaad rakhein: Daily loss limit set karein aur setup na mile toh screen se door rahein.

🚀 **FOMO Mein Aakar Green Candles Buy Karna**: Jab koi Crypto coin ya Forex pair already 20-30% pump ho chuka hota hai, tab beginners FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) mein buy kar lete hain. Bhagti hui train mein mat chadhein! Retest aur proper technical setup ka wait karein.

💡 **Bina Proper Plan Aur Journaling Ke Trading**: Trading ek business hai, koi lottery nahi. Agar aapke paas entry, exit aur Risk-to-Reward ratio ka clear plan nahi hai, toh long-term consistency namumkin hai. Apne har trade ko note karein aur apni mistakes se sikhein.

🎯 Remember, trading mein koi "sure-shot profit" ya quick magic formula nahi hota. Consistency sirf discipline, risk management aur patience se aati hai.
