Stop Loss Aur Take Profit Sahi Jagah Kaise Lagayein? Ye 3 Smart Rules Seekhein 🎯

📅 11-08-2026

💡 Kya aapka trade Take Profit (TP) hit karne se thoda pehle hi reverse ho jata hai, ya Stop Loss (SL) touch karke wapas aapke direction mein chala jata hai? Tension mat lo, ye har beginner trader ki common story hai! Stop Loss aur Take Profit sirf random lines nahi hain, balki ye aapki trading journey ka sabse bada risk management tool hain. Aaiye samajhte hain inka sahi use kaise karein.

📊 **1. Market Structure Ke Sath SL Set Karein:** Apne Stop Loss ko kabhi bhi exact Support ya Resistance level par mat rakho, kyunki market mein liquidity sweep (fake breakouts) bohot common hote hain. Sahi tareeka ye hai ke aap Key Support Level ke thoda neeche ya Resistance ke thoda upar apna SL lagayein taake market ko move karne ka room mile.

🎯 **2. Realistic TP Aur 1:2 Risk-Reward Ratio:** Lalach mein aakar TP itna door mat rakho jahan tak price ka pahunchna mushkil ho. Hamesha Next Major Structure Level par TP lagayein. Minimum 1:2 Risk-to-Reward ratio maintain karein—agar aap $10 ka risk le rahe hain, toh target kam se kam $20 hona chahiye. Isse aap long-term mein secure rahenge.

⚠️ **3. Emotional Moving Se Bachein Aur Trailing SL Use Karein:** Ek baar trade enter karne ke baad dar ke maare SL ko door mat khiskao—ye account wash hone ki sabse badi wajah hai! Iski jagah jab trade profit mein jaye, toh 'Trailing Stop Loss' ka use karke apne profit ko time-to-time lock karte jayein.
