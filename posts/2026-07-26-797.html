Stop Loss & Take Profit Set Karne Ka Sahi Tarika: Ye 3 Rules Trading Loss Kam Kar Denge 🎯

📅 26-07-2026

📊 Trading mein profit banana utna mushkil nahi hai jitna apne capital ko safe rakhna. Forex aur Crypto market mein bohot se beginners entry toh le lete hain, lekin exit plan banaye bina phas jaate hain. Stop Loss (SL) aapka safety airbag hai jo account ko wash hone se bachata hai, aur Take Profit (TP) aapka hard-earned profit right time par lock karta hai. Inka sahi use hi ek profitable trader aur amateur mein farq paida karta hai.

💡 **Stop Loss Hamesha Logic Par Lagayein:** Sabse pehle random figures (jaise strictly $10 ya 20 pips) par SL lagana chor do. Buy trade mein Key Support Zone ke thoda neeche aur Sell trade mein Resistance Zone ke thoda upar SL set karein. Market ki normal noise aur liquidity spikes se bachne ke liye hamesha Price Action aur Volatility (ATR indicator) ka sahara lein.

🎯 **Minimum 1:2 Risk-to-Reward Ratio Target Karein:** Take Profit hamesha apne risk ke hisab se set karein. Agar aap kisi trade mein $20 ka risk le rahe hain (SL), toh aapka target kam se kam $40 (TP) hona chahiye. Is disciplined rule ka faida ye hai ke agar aapki 50% trades galat bhi chali jayein, tab bhi aap overall net profit mein hi rahoge.

⚠️ **Emotional Trading Se Bachein Aur Trailing SL Use Karein:** Trade loss mein jaane par ghabra kar SL ko piche khiskana sabse badi galti hai. Plan par hamesha stick rahein! Jab market aapke favor mein move kare, toh Trailing Stop Loss ka use karein taake aapka profit gradually lock hota rahe aur risk khatam ho jaye.
