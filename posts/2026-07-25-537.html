Demo Account Se Real Account Tak Ka Safar: Sahi Tareeka Aur Smart Tips

25-07-2026

Demo trading account par profit banana kafi aasan lagta hai, lekin asli test tab hota hai jab aap Real Account par shift hote hain. Demo account mein Virtual Money hoti hai jisse koi khauf nahi hota, par real account mein aapka hard-earned capital hota hai. Yahan Darr (Fear) aur Lalach (Greed) aapke trading decisions ko control karne lagte hain.

Is transition ko smooth aur successful banane ke liye aapko in practical steps ko follow karna chahiye:

* **Small Capital Se Start Karein:** Shuruat hamesha chote amount se karein. Real account par utna hi invest karein jitna loss hone par aapki daily life par koi asar na pade.

* **Strict Risk Management:** Demo ki tarah real account mein bhi Stop Loss (SL) ka istemal zaroori hai. Ek single trade par apne total capital ka sirf 1% se 2% hi risk karein.

* **Trading Plan Follow Karein:** Market ki tezi ya mandi dekh kar panic trading mat karein. Apne setup aur strategy par stick rahein.

* **Trading Journal Banayein:** Apne har profit aur loss ka record rakhein taaki aap apni mistakes se seekh sakein aur apni strategy improve kar sakein.

Ek baat hamesha dhyan mein rakhein: Forex aur Crypto market highly volatile hote hain aur yahan koi "guaranteed profit" ya "sure-shot formula" nahi hota. Success sirf discipline, continuous learning aur proper risk management se hi milti hai.
