Demo Se Real Account Par Shift Kaise Karein? 4 Practical Tips Naye Traders Ke Liye 📈

📅 07-08-2026

📊 Demo account par virtual money se trading karna aur profits dekhna bohot easy lagta hai, lekin real account ka safar bilkul alag hota hai. Jab aapki apni hard-earned money stake par hoti hai, toh fear aur greed jaise emotions aapke decisions ko control karne lagte hain. Is transition ko smooth aur successful banane ke liye sahi mindset zaroori hai.

💡 Small capital se shuruat karein. Live market mein aate hi bada deposit karne ki galti kabhi mat karna. Sabse pehle utna hi capital deposit karein jisko lose karna aap afford kar sakein. Chote account size se aap par psychological pressure kam hoga aur aap relaxed mind ke sath apne setup par focus kar payenge.

⚠️ Risk management par strict rahein. Demo account par shayad aap bina Stop Loss ke trades chalne dete the, lekin real account par ye sabse badi galti sabit ho sakti hai. Har single trade par apni total equity ka sirf 1% se 2% risk karein. Market mein profit se zyaada apne capital ko protect karna zaroori hai.

🚀 Trading journal maintain karein. Blind trades lene ke bajaye proper technical aur fundamental analysis ka use karein. Har loss se seekhein aur apni mistakes ko note karein. Market mein koi short-cut ya guaranteed profit nahi hota, sirf aapka discipline aur risk control aapko ek consistent trader banata hai.
