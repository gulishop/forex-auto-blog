Demo Se Real Trading Main Shift Kaise Karein? 4 Practical Tips Jo Account Wash Hone Se Bachayenge 🚀

📅 26-07-2026

🧠 Demo account mein har koi profitable dikhta hai, lekin real account mein shift hotay hi game badal jata hai! Demo mein zero fear hota hai, jabki real account mein aapka hard-earned money aur emotions dono stake par hotay hain. Isliye pehla step apne mindset ko real market dynamics ke liye prepare karna hai.

⚠️ Small Size Se Start Karein: Real account mein pehle din hi bada capital mat lagayein. Hamesha minimum lot size (jaise 0.01) se shuruat karein aur apne total balance ka sirf 1% se 2% hi per trade risk karein. Demo ki tarah bade lot sizes lene ki galti initial days mein bilkul mat karein.

🎯 Strict Risk Management Rule: Real market mein bina Stop Loss ke trade open karna sabse badi galti hai. Trade lagane se pehle apna Stop Loss (SL) aur Take Profit (TP) hamesha fix karein. Mind mein rakhein ki Risk-to-Reward ratio kam se kam 1:2 hona chahiye taake losses easily recover ho sakein.

💡 Trading Journal Banayein: Demo par jo strategy work kar rahi thi, wahi real account par follow karein. Roz roz nayi indicators aur strategies try mat karein. Ek trading journal maintain karein jisme har trade ki entry, exit aur emotion note karein. FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) aur revenge trading se bachein.

*(Note: Forex aur Crypto trading mein financial market risk hota hai. Koi bhi indicator ya strategy 100% profit ki guarantee nahi deti, isliye hamesha proper risk control ke sath hi trading karein.)*
