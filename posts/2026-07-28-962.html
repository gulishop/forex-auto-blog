Demo Se Real Account Shift: Ye 4 Rules Jo Aapke Trading Capital Ko Bachayenge 📈

📅 28-07-2026

📊 Demo account par green charts dekhna easy hota hai, lekin Real account par trading shuru karte hi Darr aur Greed kyun aa jata hai? Simple answer: Real money brings real emotions! Demo account aapko strategy sikhata hai, lekin Real account aapki trading psychology aur emotional discipline ka asli test leta hai.

💡 Micro-lots se shuruat karein! Jab aap Demo se Real account par shift hon, toh pehle din hi bada capital deposit mat karein. Small lot size (jaise 0.01) aur safe risk management (1-2% per trade) ke saath shuru karein. Is se emotional pressure kam hoga aur aap apne plan par stick reh sakenge.

🎯 Apne Demo plan par stick rahein, new strategies mat try karein! Bohot se traders Real account mein aate hi panic kar ke apni proven strategy badal dete hain. Jo Stop Loss aur Risk-Reward rules aapne Demo par follow kiye the, unhe Real trading mein bhi discipline ke saath apply karein aur har trade ka Journal banayein.

⚠️ Remember, market mein koi guaranteed profit ya short-cut nahi hota! Real account par losses trading journey ka natural hissa hain. Long-term success ke liye zaruri hai ke aap risk management follow karein aur FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) se bachein.
