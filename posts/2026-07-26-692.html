Support aur Resistance Ko Pro Ki Tarah Spot Karein 🎯

📅 26-07-2026

📊 Trading charts par Support aur Resistance sabse basic lekin powerful concepts hain. Simple words mein, **Support** woh price level hai jahan market ko "floor" milta hai aur price niche girna ruk kar bounce karti hai. Wahi **Resistance** ek "ceiling" ki tarah kaam karti hai jahan price upar jaana ruk kar reject ho jati hai. Yeh zones buyers aur sellers ka asli battleground hote hain.

📈 Inhe identify karne ke liye past price history dekhein. Chart par kam se kam 2 se 3 aisi jaghein dhoondhein jahan se price ne baar baar bounce ya reject kiya ho. Un points ko connect karke horizontal lines draw karein. High accuracy ke liye hamesha higher timeframes (jaise 1H ya 4H) ka use karein, kyunki chote timeframes par market noise zyada hota hai.

💡 Pro Tip: Support aur Resistance ko single sharp line samajhne ki jagah ek **"Zone"** ki tarah draw karein. Market exact point ko touch kiye bina thoda upar-niche ho sakti hai. Is technique se aap false breakouts se bach sakte hain aur better risk-reward ratio set kar sakte hain.

⚠️ Remember, market mein koi bhi technical analysis 100% sure nahi hota aur na hi koi guaranteed profit hota hai. High volatility mein support toot kar resistance ban sakti hai. Isliye bina proper Risk Management aur Stop Loss ke kabhi trading mat karein.
