Fundamental vs Technical Analysis: Trading Mein Sahi Decision Kaise Lein? 🔍

📅 07-08-2026

📊 **Fundamental Analysis (FA) - Big Picture Ko Samjhein:**

Fundamental Analysis ka matlab hai kisi asset ki real intrinsic value ko find karna. Forex trading mein NFP news, CPI inflation data, aur Central Bank interest rate decisions FA ka hissa hote hain. Crypto mein project ki utility, team background, aur market adoption ko dekha jata hai. Ye method aapko batata hai ke market kis direction mein *kyun* move kar sakti hai. Agar aap long-term investor hain, toh FA aapke liye behad zaroori hai.

📈 **Technical Analysis (TA) - Charts Aur Price Action Ki Power:**

Technical Analysis purely charts, historical price movements, candlestick patterns, aur technical indicators (jaise RSI, Moving Averages) par depend karta hai. TA aapko ye batata hai ke trade mein enter ya exit *kab* hona hai. Day traders aur scalpers ke liye TA seekhna bohot zaroori hai kyunki ye short-term price movements aur key support/resistance levels spot karne mein madad karta hai.

💡 **Pro Tip: Success Dono Ke Combination Mein Hai!**

Naye traders aksar puchte hain: "FA behtar hai ya TA?" Real secret ye hai ke dono ka combo sabse powerful hota hai! Fundamental Analysis aapko market ka trend aur direction samajhne mein help karta hai, jabke Technical Analysis aapko perfect entry, stop-loss, aur target points set karne mein guide karta hai. Smart traders news aur charts dono par nazar rakhte hain.
