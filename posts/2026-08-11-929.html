Support Aur Resistance Sahi Tarike Se Kaise Dhoondhein? Ye 3 Simple Steps Try Karein 📈

📅 11-08-2026

📊 Trading charts par Support aur Resistance aise key levels hote hain jo aapki trading accuracy ko double kar sakte hain. Support ko ek "floor" ki tarah samjhein jahan se price niche girne ke baad bounce back karti hai. Jab bhi market kisi price point par aakar baar baar rukti hai aur wahan se buyers active ho jate hain, toh samajh jayein ki wo aapka strong Support zone hai.

🎯 Dusri taraf, Resistance market ki "ceiling" ya chhat hoti hai. Jab price upar ki taraf move karti hai aur ek specific level ko touch karke baar baar rejection face karti hai, toh wahan sellers dominant ho jate hain. Ek simple rule yaad rakhein: Jitni baar price kisi level ko test karke reverse hoti hai, wo Support ya Resistance level utna hi strong mana jata hai.

💡 Isko charts par spot karne ke liye 1-Hour ya 4-Hour timeframe use karein. Chart par pichle major Swing Highs aur Swing Lows ko dhoondhein. Ek single line khinchne ke bajaye hamesha ek "Zone" (price range) mark karein, kyunki market exact single price ko hamesha respect nahi karti. Candle wicks aur rejection shadows aapko sahi zone mark karne mein help karenge.
