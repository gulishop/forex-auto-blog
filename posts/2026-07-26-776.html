90% Traders Loss Kyun Karte Hain? Trading Psychology Ke 3 Golden Rules Sikhein 🧠

📅 26-07-2026

⚠️ Strategy chahe kitni bhi zabardast ho, agar aapka apne emotions par control nahi hai toh account wash hote waqt nahi lagta! Market mein trading charts se zyada aapke mindset ka khel hota hai. Trading ke do sabse bade villains hain—**FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out)** aur **Greed (Lalach)**. Jab aap green candles ko dekh kar jaldbazi mein bina kisi solid setup ke entry lete ho, toh aap trading nahi balki gambling kar rahe hote ho.

📉 Ek aur sabse badi galti hoti hai **Revenge Trading**. Loss hone ke baad gusse mein aakar "aaj hi loss recover karna hai" ki soch aapko aur bade financial loss mein phenk deti hai. Isse bachne ka ek hi tarika hai: har trade par strict Stop Loss (SL) aur Take Profit (TP) use karein. Jab aapka exit level pehle se decide hoga, toh market ke ups & downs aapko emotional panic nahi denge.

💡 Professional trader banne ke liye Discipline sabse zaroori hai. Ek **Trading Journal** banayein aur har trade ka reason note karein—kya aapne rules follow kiye ya emotion mein decision liya? Market hamesha aapke according nahi chalegi, lekin apne risk ko manage karna completely aapke haath mein hota hai. Consistency hamesha patience aur discipline se aati hai, over-trading se nahi.

🛡️ Trading mein high risk hota hai aur koi bhi strategy 100% guaranteed profit nahi deti. Market volatility ko samjhein aur sirf wohi capital risk par lagayein jisko aap loss karna afford kar sakte hain. Profit ke peeche bhaagne ke bajaye process aur risk management par focus karein.
