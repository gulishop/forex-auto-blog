Chart Par Support Aur Resistance Sahi Tarike Se Kaise Dhoondein? Seekho Ye 3 Practical Tips 🎯

📅 26-07-2026

💡 Trading chart par Support aur Resistance samajhna bilkul market ka 'floor' aur 'ceiling' dhoondne jaisa hai. Support wo price level hai jahan buyers active hote hain aur price bounce karti hai, jabki Resistance wo zone hai jahan sellers dominant ho jate hain aur price ko neeche push karte hain. In key levels ko master karna har Forex aur Crypto trader ke liye sabse pehla aur important step hai.

📊 **1. Historical Price Rejections Dhoondo**: Chart par piche jaakar wo spots dekhein jahan price ne kam se kam 2 ya 3 baar strong bounce ya rejection liya ho. Jitne zyadah touches honge, wo level utna hi strong maana jayega. Always higher timeframes (jaise 1H, 4H ya Daily) se start karein taake clear picture mile.

🎯 **2. Exact Line Nahi, Always Mark a Zone**: Naye traders aksar ek single line draw karte hain, jo sabse badi galti hai. Market hamesha precise nahi hoti, isliye ek single line ki bajaye hamesha "Support/Resistance Area or Zone" mark karein. Isse aap market ke fakeouts se bach sakein ge.

🔄 **3. Flip/Role Reversal Ko Samjho**: Jab ek strong Resistance breakout hoti hai, toh future move ke liye wo naya Support ban jaati hai. Waise hi agar Support break ho jaye, toh wo Resistance ban jata hai. Breakout ke baad Retest ka wait karna sabse high-probability entry strategy hoti hai.

⚠️ **Risk Control & Reality Check**: Trading mein koi bhi strategy 100% guaranteed profit nahi deti aur market kisi bhi waqt trend badal sakti hai. In levels par blindly trade lene ke bajaye hamesha candlestick patterns, volume confirmation aur strict Risk Management ka use karein.
