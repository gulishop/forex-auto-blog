Forex Trading Mein Account Wash Hone Se Kaise Bachayein? Ye 3 Golden Risk Management Rules Sikhein 🛡️

📅 29-07-2026

💰 **1-2% Per Trade Rule Ko Hamesha Follow Karein:** Naye traders ki sabse badi galti hoti hai FOMO mein aakar bada lot size lena. Hamesha yaad rakhein, ek single trade mein apne total account balance ka sirf 1% se 2% hi risk karein. Agar aapka account $1,000 ka hai, toh aapka max loss $10-$20 se zyada nahi hona chahiye. Is simple rule se aapka capital hamesha safe rahega.

🎯 **Stop Loss Ko Apna Best Friend Banayein:** Bina Stop Loss (SL) ke trade lagana matlab bina brakes ke gaadi chalana! Trade enter karne se pehle hi apna exit point decide karein. Jab SL hit ho jaaye, toh emotionally market se badla lene ke bajaye loss ko accept karein. Capital bachega, tabhi toh aap agli trade laga paayenge!

📊 **Risk-to-Reward Ratio (RRR) Sahi Rakhein:** Hamesha kam se kam 1:2 Risk-to-Reward ratio par kaam karein. Iska matlab hai agar aap $10 ka risk le rahe hain, toh aapka profit target kam se kam $20 hona chahiye. Is strategy ki wajah se agar aapki 50% trades galat bhi ho jayein, tab bhi aap end of the month profit mein rahenge.

⚠️ **Revenge Trading Aur Overtrading Se Door Rahein:** Ek ya do stop loss hit hone par aggressive trades mat kholein. Market roz khulti hai, har candle par trade lena zaroori nahi hota. Proper risk management aur discipline hi ek successful trader ki asli pehchan hai.
