🧠 Trading Psychology: FOMO Aur Fear Se Apne Account Ko Tabah Hone Se Kaise Bachayein?

📅 13-08-2026

📊 Kya aapke saath kabhi aisa hua hai ke zabardast chart analysis aur indicators ke bawajood aapka trade loss mein chala gaya? Modern trading world ka sabse bada sach ye hai ke trading 20% technical strategy hai aur 80% psychological mind control! Jab tak aap apne emotions—jaise lalach (greed) aur darr (fear)—ko control nahi karte, tab tak market mein profit banana taqriban namumkin hai.

⚠️ Naye traders ke do sabse bade dushman hote hain: FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) aur Revenge Trading. Pump hoti hui green candles dekh kar bina kisi plan ke impulsive entry lena, ya loss hone ke gusse mein over-leveraged trade lagana aapke capital ko zero kar sakta hai. Successful traders kabhi emotions par trading nahi karte, wo sirf apne system ko execute karte hain.

💡 Apne emotions ko master karne ke liye ye 3 practical rules follow karein:

1. Entry lene se pehle Stop Loss (SL) zaroor set karein taake market move se aapko sudden panic na ho.

2. Har trade par sirf 1-2% capital ka risk lein—utna hi risk lein jitna loss hone par aap raat ko sukoon se so sakein.

3. Ek Trading Journal banayein aur note karein ke kaunsa emotion (darr ya lalach) aapko galti karne par majboor kar raha hai.

🎯 Trading koi "raato-raat ameer banne" ki scheme nahi hai, balke ye discipline, patience aur risk management ka game hai. Market mein koi guarantee nahi hoti, lekin agar aap apne emotions par control rakhenge toh long-term mein ek profitable trader zaroor ban sakte hain.
