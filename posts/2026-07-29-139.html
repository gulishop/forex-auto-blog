Fundamental vs Technical Analysis: Naye Traders Ke Liye Kaun Sa Best Hai? (Sahi Tarika Samjhein) 📊

📅 29-07-2026

💡 Trading ki dunya mein step in karte hi sabse pehla sawal yehi aata hai: Charts dekhein ya News par focus karein? Fundamental Analysis (FA) ka matlab hai kisi bhi asset ki real value aur uski overall health ko samajhna. Crypto mein project ki utility, team aur adoption rate dekha jata hai, jabki Forex mein Interest Rates, Inflation aur NFP jaise economic data par nazar rakhi jati hai. FA aapko ye samjhata hai ke *WHAT* to trade — yani kaun sa coin ya currency pair strong hai.

📈 Dusri taraf, Technical Analysis (TA) poori tarah se Price Action aur Historical Charts par depend karta hai. Yahan aap Support/Resistance levels, Candlestick patterns aur Technical Indicators (jaise RSI ya MACD) ki madad se market ki future movement ka andaza lagate hain. TA ka main maqsad ye batana hai ke trade mein *WHEN* to enter aur exit — yani sahi timing kya hai.

🎯 Toh sawal ye hai ke aapko kaun sa tarika chunna chahiye? Pro traders kabhi bhi sirf ek cheez par rely nahi karte. Sabse smart strategy yehi hai ke aap dono ko combine karein! Fundamental analysis se market ki overall direction pakdein aur Technical analysis se exact entry point dhoondlein. Hamesha yaad rakhein ke trading mein koi "sure profit" formula nahi hota, isliye Proper Risk Management aur Stop Loss ka istemal zaroor karein.
