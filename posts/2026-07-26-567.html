🎯 Step-by-Step Guide: Apna Winning Trading Plan Kaise Banayein?

📅 26-07-2026

📊 **Clear Trading Goals & Risk Setup:** Forex aur Crypto market mein bina plan ke entry lena matlab blind gambling karna hai! Ek pro-trader banne ke liye sabse pehle apne financial goals aur Risk Management ko define karo. Always decide karo ki aap per trade apne capital ka kitna percent risk lene ko tayyar ho (ideally 1% se 2%). Risk ko pehle se accept karna hi trading discipline ki pehli seorhi hai.

📈 **Solid Entry & Exit Strategy:** Tumhara chart analysis kitna bhi achha ho, jab tak tumhare paas strict rules nahi hain, aap market mein long-term survive nahi kar sakte. Technical analysis, price action ya indicators ke base par apni exact entry point, Stop-Loss (SL) aur Take-Profit (TP) humesha trade lene se pehle fix karo. Market mein random trades ya FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) se door raho.

💡 **Trading Journal & Risk Disclaimer:** Trading ek continuous learning game hai. Daily apne sabhi trades ko ek journal mein write down karo—dekho konsa setup kaam kar raha hai aur kahan galti hui. Ek baat hamesha yaad rakho, trading market volatile hoti hai aur yahan koi "sure-shot profit" ya guaranteed success nahi hoti. Risk har trade mein hota hai, isliye humesha responsible ho kar trade karein aur emotional decisions mat lein.
