📊 4 Essential Candlestick Patterns Jo Har Trader Ko Pata Hone Chahiye (Aur Inka Sahi Use)

📅 29-07-2026

🚀 Trading charts ko dekh kar confuse ho jaate ho? Tension mat lo! Candlestick patterns market ki psychology samajhne ka sabse powerful tool hain. Agar aap Forex ya Crypto market mein naye ho, toh ye basic patterns aapki chart reading skills ko zaroor improve karenge.

💡 **Hammer & Bullish Engulfing (Buying Signals)**: Jab market downtrend mein ho aur niche ek Hammer candle bane (lambi niche wali wick aur choti body), toh ye bullish reversal ka ishara hota hai. Wahi Bullish Engulfing tab banti hai jab ek badi green candle pichli red candle ko poori tarah cover kar le, jo dikhati hai ke buyers market mein wapas aa chuke hain.

📉 **Shooting Star & Bearish Engulfing (Selling Signals)**: Uptrend ke top par jab ek lambi upper shadow wali Shooting Star candle bane, toh samajh jao ke buyers thak chuke hain aur sellers pressure bana rahe hain. Iske ilawa, Bearish Engulfing pattern tab banta hai jab ek badi red candle pichli green candle ko swallow kar leti hai, jo price girne ka signal deti hai.

🎯 **Doji (Market Indecision)**: Doji candle tab banti hai jab opening aur closing price lagbhag same ho (+ shape jaisi). Ye batati hai ke buyers aur sellers ke beech kante ki takkar hai aur market confuse hai. Jab bhi Doji dekho, furan trade lene ke bajaye agli candle ke confirmation ka wait karna sabse samajhdari hai.

⚠️ **Pro Tip & Risk Management**: Yaad rakhein, koi bhi pattern 100% profit ki guarantee nahi deta! In patterns ko hamesha Support/Resistance zones aur indicators ke saath combine karke hi trade karein. Risk ko control mein rakhne ke liye har trade par Stop Loss zaroor lagayein.
