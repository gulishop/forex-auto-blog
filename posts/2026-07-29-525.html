Crypto Market Ki Volatility Ko Kaise Handle Karein? Ye 3 Practical Tips Seekhein 📈

📅 29-07-2026

📊 **Volatility Kya Hai Aur Ye Kyun Hoti Hai?**

Crypto market mein prices minutes mein rapid move karti hain. Is fast price swing ko hi market volatility kehte hain. 24/7 trading, sudden news updates aur whale movements iski sabse badi wajahein hain. Dhyan rakhein, volatility sirf risk nahi laati, balki agar aapke paas sahi strategy ho toh ye profit banane ke behtareen mauke bhi deti hai!

🎯 **Risk Management Ko Apni Top Priority Banayein**

Volatile market mein survive karne ke liye Stop-Loss (SL) aapka sabse bada weapon hai. Har trade par apne total capital ka sirf 1-2% risk karein. Leverage hamesha low (3x-5x) rakhein taake market ki sudden wicks aapke account ko liquidate na kar sakein. Proper position sizing hi aapko bada loss hone se bachati hai.

💡 **FOMO Se Bachein Aur Indicators Ka Sahi Use Karein**

Jab price tez spike le rahi ho, toh FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) mein aakar jump mat karein. Market ki volatility ko measure karne ke liye ATR (Average True Range) aur Bollinger Bands jaise technical indicators ka use karein. Green candles dekh kar excited hone ki bajaye apni entry aur exit pehle se plan karke rakhein.
