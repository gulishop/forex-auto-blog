Forex Mein Account Safe Rakhne Ke 3 Golden Rules (Loss Se Kaise Bachein?) 🛡️

📅 27-07-2026

📈 Kya aapka account bhi baar baar wash ho jata hai? Trust me, Forex trading mein profit kamane se zyada zaroori apne capital ko protect karna hai. Successful traders ka secret koi magic indicator nahi, balki solid Risk Management hai. Agar aap risk control karna sikh gaye, toh market mein lambi race ke ghode ban sakte ho!

🎯 **1% Se 2% Per Trade Rule Follow Karo:** Kabhi bhi ek single trade mein apne total account ka 1% ya 2% se zyada risk mat lo. Agar aapka balance $1,000 hai, toh ek trade mein maximum $10-$20 ka risk banta hai. Is strategy se agar aapki continuous 4-5 trades loss mein bhi chali jayein, tab bhi aapka account secure rahega.

⚠️ **Stop Loss (SL) Ke Bina Trade Mat Karo:** Trade open karne se pehle apna exit point hamesha set karo. Stop Loss aapka personal bodyguard hai jo aapko emotional decisions aur unexpected market moves se bachata. System mein SL lagana chart dekh kar man mein SL rakhne se 100 guna behtar hai.

📊 **Minimum 1:2 Risk-to-Reward Ratio Rakho:** Hamesha aisi trades setup karo jahan aapka profit potential aapke risk se double ho. Agar aap $10 risk kar rahe ho, toh target kam se kam $20 profit ka hona chahiye. Is math ke sath agar aapki 50% trades bhi galat hongi, tab bhi aap overall green mein rahoge!

💡 *Disclaimer:* Forex market mein trading high risk wali hoti hai aur koi bhi setup 100% profit ki guarantee nahi deta. Smart traders hamesha discipline aur patience ke sath trade karte hain.
