Leverage Kya Hai? 3 Simple Rules Jo Aapke Trading Account Ko Zero Hone Se Bachayenge 🛡️

📅 30-07-2026

📊 Trading ki duniya mein **Leverage** ek aisi superpower hai jo aapke chote capital ko badi trades lene ki permission deti hai. Simply put, ye broker ki taraf se mila ek tarah ka temporary loan hota hai. Agar aapke paas $100 hain aur aap 1:10 leverage use karte ho, toh aap market mein $1,000 ki trade open kar sakte ho. Ye aapke potential profits ko multi-fold kar deta hai—lekin yaad rakhein, ye ek double-edged sword hai!

⚠️ **1. High Leverage Ka Jaal:** Naye traders aksar jaldi ameer banne ke chakkar mein 1:100 ya 1:500 jaisi high leverage select kar lete hain. Result? Market ka ek chota sa galat move aur poora account wash! As a beginner, hamesha low leverage (jaise 1:5 ya 1:10) se shuruat karein taake aapka account market ki volatility ko bardaasht kar sake.

🎯 **2. Stop Loss Ko Mandatory Banayein:** Jab aap leverage use kar rahe hon, toh Stop Loss (SL) lagana optional nahi, balki zaroori hai. Rule ye rakhein ke kisi bhi ek trade mein apne total wallet balance ka 1% se 2% se zyada risk na lein. Correct Risk-to-Reward ratio hi aapko long-term trader banata hai.

💡 **3. Emotional Trading Se Bachein:** High leverage ke sath trades execute karte waqt over-trading ya revenge trading se parhez karein. Leverage market mein koi quick-money scheme ya jackpot nahi hai, balki ek risk management tool hai jise discipline ke sath use karna hota hai. Trading mein koi profit guaranteed nahi hota, isliye hamesha smart risk strategy apnaayein.
