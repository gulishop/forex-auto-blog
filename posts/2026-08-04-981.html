Trading Psychology: 3 Emotional Galtiyan Jo Aapka Profit Kha Jati Hain (Aur Unse Kaise Bachein) 🧠

📅 04-08-2026

📉 Kya aapke sath kabhi aisa hua hai ke bilkul sahi chart analysis ke bawajood aapka trade loss mein chala gaya? Sach ye hai ke trading mein sirf 20% technical analysis hota hai, baki 80% aapki Trading Psychology kaam karti hai. FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) aur Greed wo do sabse bade dushman hain jo acche se acche setup ko bhi loss mein badal dete hain.

💡 Apne emotions par kabu paane aur ek disciplined trader banne ke liye ye 3 practical rules aaj se hi follow karein:

🎯 **Fixed Risk Discipline:** Har trade mein apne total capital ka sirf 1-2% risk par rakhein. Jab loss ka amount pehle se pata hoga, toh darr bilkul nahi lagega.

🛑 **Stop-Loss Respect:** Agar aapka Stop-Loss hit ho jaye, toh market se badla lene ki koshish mat karein. Loss ko accept karein aur agli acchi opportunity ka wait karein.

🚫 **No Revenge Trading:** Loss hone ke baad turant emotional hokar doosra trade mat lagayein. Market se fight karna hamesha account wash karwata hai.

🚀 Kamyab traders wo nahi jo har trade jeette hain, balki wo hain jo apne mind ko calm aur disciplined rakhte hain. Trading ek marathon hai, koi overnight rich hone ki scheme nahi, isliye process par focus karein.
