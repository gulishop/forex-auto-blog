🚨 4 Badi Galtiyan Jo Naye Traders Karte Hain (Aur Inse Kaise Bachein)

📅 28-07-2026

Trading ki duniya exciting lagti hai, lekin initial days mein aksar beginners kuch common mistakes karke apna hard-earned capital lose kar dete hain. Agar aap Forex ya Crypto market mein naye hain, toh in 4 galtiyon se bachna aapke trading journey ko bohot smooth bana sakta hai:

⚠️ **1. High Leverage aur Poor Risk Management:**

Naye traders quick profit ke chakkar mein high leverage use karte hain. Always remember, capital save karna pehla rule hai! Apne total account balance ka sirf 1-2% hi ek trade mein risk karein. Smart risk management hi aapko long run mein market mein tikaye rakhti hai.

🎯 **2. Stop Loss (SL) Na Lagana:**

"Market zaroor wapas aayega" ye soch kar bina Stop Loss ke trade open rakhna sabse bada trap hai. Entry lene se pehle hi apna risk aur reward level decide karein. Chart par SL hamesha set rakhein aur trade ke dauran emotional hokar usko shift mat karein.

🚀 **3. FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) Trading:**

Jab koi coin ya currency pair already pump kar chuka hota hai, toh naye traders FOMO mein aakar bina kisi setup ke buy kar lete hain. Hamesha apne trading plan aur confirmations ka wait karein. Market roz open hoti hai, sahi opportunity ka wait karna seekhein.

📊 **4. Over-trading aur Revenge Trading:**

Ek loss hone ke baad, us loss ko turant recover karne ke liye emotional trades lena 'Revenge Trading' kehlata hai. Market se badla lene ki koshish hamesha aur bade losses karwati hai. Din mein 2 loss ho jayein toh screen off karke break lena hi smart decision hai.

💡 **Important Tip:** Trading mein koi guaranteed profits ya overnight success nahi hoti, market hamesha risk ke sath aati hai. Success sirf discipline, risk control aur continuous learning se milti hai.
