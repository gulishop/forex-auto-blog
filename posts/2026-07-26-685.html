🕯️ Yeh 4 Candlestick Patterns Apko Loss Se Bachayein Ge (Aur Next Move Predict Karein Ge)

📅 26-07-2026

📈 Trading charts ko dekh kar confuse ho jate hain? Relax, aap akele nahi hain! Candlestick patterns asal mein market ki apni language hain. Jab aap in shapes aur wicks ko samajhne lagte hain, toh Forex aur Crypto market ka agla potential move pakadna kafi easy ho jata hai. Aaiye dekhte hain wo top candlestick patterns jo har trader ki watchlist mein honi chahiye.

🎯 **1. Hammer Pattern (Bullish Reversal)**

Jab market downtrend mein ho aur niche ek choti body aur lambi wick wali candle bane, toh use Hammer kehte hain. Ye signal deta hai ke sellers ne price ko drop karne ki koshish ki, lekin buyers ne market mein enter ho kar control wapas le liya. Strong support level par Hammer dikhe toh ek bullish rebound ki ummeed hoti hai.

⚠️ **2. Shooting Star (Bearish Reversal)**

Ye Hammer ka exact opposite hai aur hamesha uptrend ke top par banta hai. Iski lambi upper wick batati hai ke buyers price ko upar le gaye the, lekin sellers ne aggressive selling karke price ko niche close karwaya. Ye potential market drop ya correction ka early warning sign hai.

🔥 **3. Bullish & Bearish Engulfing**

Jab ek badi candle apni pichli choti candle ko poori tarah cover (engulf) kar le, toh isko Engulfing pattern bolte hain. Ye strong momentum shift ko darshata hai. Red ke baad badi Green candle aaye toh Bullish Engulfing, aur Green ke baad badi Red candle aaye toh Bearish Engulfing banta hai.

💡 **Pro Tip & Risk Disclaimer**

Yaad rakhein, chart par koi bhi pattern 100% profit ki guarantee nahi deta! Trading mein risk hamesha rehta hai. Isliye hamesha confirmation ke sath trade karein—jaise volume check karna ya support/resistance levels ko dekhna. Risk management aur Stop Loss ke bina kabhi bhi koi trade execute mat karein.
