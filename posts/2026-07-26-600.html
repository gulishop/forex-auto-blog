🛡️ Account Blow Hone Se Kaise Bachayein? Forex Risk Management Ke 4 Golden Rules

📅 26-07-2026

📊 **1-2% Rule Ko Apna Best Friend Banayein:** Sabse pehle ye baat samajhna zaroori hai ke Forex trading mein capital save rakhna pehla step hai. Apne total account balance ka sirf 1% se 2% hi ek trade mein risk par lagayein. Agar aapke paas $1,000 ka account hai, toh ek trade par $10-$20 se ziada risk mat lein. Is tarah agar consecutive losses bhi aayein, tab bhi aapka account safe rahega.

🎯 **Stop Loss Ke Bina Trade Bilkul Mat Karein:** Trading bina Stop Loss ke bilkul waise hi hai jaise bina seatbelt ke fast car chalana! Apni trade entry lene se pehle hi exit point decide karein. Market analysis ke hisab se proper technical levels par Stop Loss lagayein taake unexpected market spikes aapka poora account wash na kar dein.

📈 **Smart Risk-to-Reward Ratio Set Karein:** Hamesha kam se kam 1:2 ka Risk-to-Reward ratio maintain karein. Matlab agar aap $10 ka risk le rahe hain, toh profit target kam se kam $20 hona chahiye. Iska sabse bada benefit ye hai ke agar aapki half trades loss mein bhi chali jayein, tab bhi aap overall profitable hi rahoge.

💡 **Emotions Control Karein Aur Over-Trading Se Bachein:** Loss hone ke baad revenge trading karna aur emotional ho kar bari lot size use karna naye traders ki sabse badi galti hoti hai. Market aapke emotions ko nahi dekhti. Strict daily loss limit set karein aur target hit hone par screen off kar dein.
