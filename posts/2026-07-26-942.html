Leverage Se Trading Account Wash Hoga Ya Profit Multiplied? Jaaniye Isse Safely Use Karne Ke 3 Golden Rules 💡

📅 26-07-2026

📊 Trading world mein **Leverage** ek aisa powerful tool hai jo aapke chote capital ko badi buying power deta hai. Aasan words mein kahein toh, agar aapke account mein sirf $100 hain aur aap 1:100 ka leverage use karte ho, toh aap market mein $10,000 tak ki position open kar sakte ho. Ye feature aapke potential profits ko multiply kar deta hai, lekin iska matlab ye bilkul nahi hai ki ye koi free money hai!

⚠️ Hamesha yaad rakhein—leverage ek **Double-Edged Sword** (dohari talwar) hai. Jitni speed se isme profit dikhta hai, utni hi tezi se aapka poora trading account wash (liquidate) bhi ho sakta hai. High leverage jaise 1:500 ya 1:1000 new traders ke liye sabse bada trap hai. Bina proper risk management ke high leverage use karna matlab bina brakes ke gaadi chalana hai.

🎯 Isko safely handle karne ke 3 simple aur practical rules follow karein:

• **Low Leverage Choose Karein:** Beginners ko max 1:10 ya 1:20 leverage se hi start karna chahiye.

• **Stop Loss Zaroor Lagayein:** Market ke sudden moves se apne capital ko bachane ke liye Stop Loss compulsory hai.

• **Proper Position Sizing:** Kisi bhi ek trade par apne total trading capital ka 1% se 2% se zyada risk kabhi mat lein.

💡 **Pro Tip:** Successful trader woh nahi jo ek din mein account double kare, balki woh hai jo apne capital ko protect karke long-term market mein tikka rahe. Trading mein guaranteed profit jaisa kuch nahi hota, sab kuch aapki analysis aur risk management par depend karta hai.
