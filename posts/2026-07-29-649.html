Forex Trading Mein Capital Blow Hone Se Kaise Bachayein? Ye 3 Simple Risk Management Rules Seekho 🛡️

📅 29-07-2026

💰 **1-2% Per Trade Rule Set Karo:** Trading mein sabse badi galti hoti hai over-leveraging aur greed. Hamesha apne total account balance ka sirf 1% se 2% tak hi ek single trade mein risk par lagao. Agar aapka account balance $1,000 hai, toh ek trade par maximum $10-$20 se zyada risk nahi lena chahiye. Ye simple rule aapko bade loss se bachayega aur continuous losses ke baad bhi aap market mein ticke rahoge.

🎯 **Stop Loss Ko Apna Financial Seatbelt Banao:** Market kitni bhi clear kyun na lage, Stop Loss (SL) lagaye bina kabhi trade enter mat karo. Market unpredictable hoti hai aur seconds mein turn le sakti hai. Trade open karne se pehle apna exit point decide karo. Sabse zaroori baat: Trade loss mein jane par apne SL ko emotions mein aakar drag ya remove bilkul mat karo.

📊 **Minimum 1:2 Risk-to-Reward Ratio Follow Karo:** Hamesha aisi trade setup pick karo jahan aapka profit potential aapke risk se kam se kam double ho. Yani agar aap $10 risk kar rahe ho, toh target kam se kam $20 ka hona chahiye. Is ratio ka faida ye hai ke agar aapki 50% trades galat bhi ho jayein, tab bhi aap month end par overall overall profit mein rahoge.

⚠️ **Discipline Is Everything:** Trading koi get-rich-quick scheme nahi hai, ye ek proper business hai. Yahan successful trader woh nahi jo ek raat mein account double kare, balki woh hai jo apne capital ko safe rakh kar long-term growth hasil kare. Profit chase karne se pehle hamesha apna risk calculate karo.
