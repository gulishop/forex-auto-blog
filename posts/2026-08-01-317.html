Trading Plan Kaise Banayein? Ye 4 Simple Steps Aapko Disciplined Trader Banayenge 📈

📅 01-08-2026

🎯 **Step 1: Risk Management Aur Capital Limit Decide Karein**

Bina plan ke trading karna matlab bina map ke jungle mein jana! Sabse pehle apna trading capital set karein aur decide karein ke aap ek single trade par kitna risk le sakte hain. Professional traders hamesha 1% se 2% per trade risk rule follow karte hain. Agar aapka account $1000 ka hai, toh ek trade par $10-$20 se zyada risk mat lein taake aapka account safe rahe.

💡 **Step 2: Clear Entry, Exit Aur Risk-to-Reward Set Karein**

Trade lene se pehle hi aapko pata hona chahiye ke aapka Stop Loss (SL) aur Take Profit (TP) kahan hoga. Hamesha minimum 1:2 Risk-to-Reward ratio maintain karein. Iska matlab agar aap $10 lose karne ko tayyar hain, toh target kam se kam $20 profit ka hona chahiye. Random market movements par jump karna band karein!

📊 **Step 3: Daily Trading Journal Maintain Karein**

Trading mein consistency indicator se zyada aapke discipline se aati hai. Apni har trade ki entry, exit, aur reason ek notebook ya Excel sheet mein record karein. Isse aapko apni mistakes track karne mein madad milegi aur aap FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) aur revenge trading se bach sakeinge.

⚠️ **Pro Tip & Risk Disclaimer**

Yaad rakhein, Crypto aur Forex markets highly volatile aur risky hain. Market mein koi bhi strategy ya plan 100% sure profit ki guarantee nahi deta. Ek achha trading plan aapko raato-raat ameer nahi banata, balki aapke risk ko control karke long-term survival mein help karta hai.
