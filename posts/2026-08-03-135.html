4 Badi Galtiyan Jo Naye Forex Aur Crypto Traders Karte Hain 🛑 (Aur Inse Kaise Bachein)

📅 03-08-2026

Trading ki duniya mein har beginner quick profit banane ke sapne le kar aata hai, lekin proper knowledge ke bina 90% log apna capital lose kar dete hain. Agar aap apni trading journey shuru kar rahe hain, toh in common mistakes se bachna sabse zaroori hai:

⚠️ **High Leverage Aur Risk Management Ki Kami:** Naye traders account double karne ke chakkar mein 50x ya 100x leverage use karte hain. Result? Ek choti si opposite candle aur account wash! Always remember, ek single trade mein apne total capital ka sirf 1-2% hi risk karein aur Stop Loss zaroor lagayein.

🚀 **FOMO Mein Aakar Green Candles Chase Karna:** Jab koi crypto coin ya currency pair already 20% boom kar chuka hota hai, tab beginners 'Fear Of Missing Out' (FOMO) ki waja se top par buy kar lete hain. Late entry humesha loss deti hai, isliye chart ko chase mat karein aur proper retracement ka wait karein.

🎯 **Revenge Trading Aur Emotional Decision:** Ek trade mein loss hua aur usko recover karne ke liye turant bina analysis ke doosri badi trade open kar di? Isse Revenge Trading kehte hain. Market se badla lene ki koshish mat karein, apne daily loss limit par stick rahein.

💡 **Bina Trading Plan Aur Journal Ke Trading:** Bina stop loss aur take-profit target ke trade lagana blind driving jaisa hai. Hamesha apna trading plan banayein aur ek trading journal maintain karein taaki aap apni mistakes se seekh sakein.

Remember, trading mein profit ki koi 100% guarantee nahi hoti aur ye risky bhi ho sakti hai, lekin right discipline aur risk management ke saath aap long-term mein grow kar sakte hain.
