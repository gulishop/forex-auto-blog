Fundamental Vs Technical Analysis: Aapke Liye Konsa Trading Style Sahi Hai? 📈💡

📅 28-07-2026

📰 Trading start karte hi sabse bada confusion hota hai—charts dekhne chahiye ya market ki news? Fundamental Analysis (FA) ka matlab hai kisi bhi asset ki real value aur uski "health" ko samajhna. Forex mein Interest Rates, GDP aur CPI data, jabki Crypto mein project ki utility, team aur adoption ko dekha jata hai. Simple words mein, FA aapko batata hai ke "WHY" (kyun) kisi asset mein invest karna chahiye. Ye long-term position ke liye best mana jata hai.

📊 Dusri taraf hai Technical Analysis (TA), jahan sara khel Price Action, Charts, Support/Resistance aur Indicators (jaise RSI aur Moving Averages) ka hota hai. TA market ke past trends aur chart patterns ko dekh kar future price movement ka andaza lagati hai. Ye aapko batati hai ke exact "WHEN" (kab) buy ya sell karna hai. Day traders aur short-term swing traders ke liye TA sabse zaroori tool hai.

💡 Toh dono mein se konsa behtar hai? Pro tip ye hai ke smart traders dono ka combination use karte hain! Pehle FA se ek solid currency pair ya crypto coin pick karein, aur phir TA ki madad se perfect entry aur exit timing set karein. Lekin yaad rakhein, trading market volatile hoti hai aur koi bhi analysis 100% sure profit ki guarantee nahi deta. Hamesha proper Risk Management aur Stop Loss ke sath hi trade karein.
