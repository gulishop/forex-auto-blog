🛡️ Forex Trading Mein Capital Safe Rakhne Ke 3 Golden Rules (Jo Aapko Bada Loss Hone Se Bachayenge)

📅 13-08-2026

📊 Trading mein success ka sabse bada secret sirf profit kamana nahi, balki apne capital ko protect karna hai! Bohot se beginners market mein enter hote hi big profits ke chakkar mein bina kisi risk plan ke trading karte hain aur akhir mein apna account blow-up kar dete hain. Trading ek marathon hai, 100m sprint nahi—isliye market mein tikka rehna sabse zaroori hai.

⚠️ **1% - 2% Risk Rule Follow Karein**: Ek single trade par apne total account balance ka sirf 1% se 2% hi risk par lagayein. Example ke taur par, agar aapka account balance $1,000 hai, toh ek trade mein maximum $10 se $20 ka loss bear karne ki capacity rakhein. Is strategy se agar aapko continuous 3-4 losses bhi ho jayein, tab bhi aapka account safe rahega aur aap relaxed feel karenge.

🎯 **Stop Loss (SL) Ke Bina Trade Mat Karein**: Stop loss lagaye bina trade karna bilkul aisa hai jaise bina brakes ke car chalana. Trade open karne se pehle hi apna exit point decide karein. Market volatility se bachne ke liye hamesha technical support ya resistance levels ke mutabiq Disciplined Stop Loss set karein, aur emotional ho kar SL ko baar-baar shift mat karein.

💡 **Minimum 1:2 Risk-to-Reward Ratio Set Karein**: Hamesha aisi trades search karein jahan potential reward aapke risk se kam se kam double ho. Agar aap $10 risk kar rahe hain, toh profit target kam se kam $20 hona chahiye. Iska faida ye hai ke agar aapki 50% trades galat bhi ho jayein, tab bhi aap net overall profit mein hi rahenge.

🚀 Remember, Forex market highly volatile hoti hai aur yahan koi guaranteed profits ya "100% win rate" jaisa shortcut nahi hota. Consistent success sirf strict risk management aur discipline se hi milti hai.
