Trading Plan Kaise Banayein? Ye 4 Simple Steps Aapko Consistent Trader Banane Mein Help Karenge 🎯

📅 29-07-2026

💡 Trading bina plan ke karna matlab bina map ke kisi anjaaye safar par nikalna hai! Forex aur Crypto markets kaafi volatile hoti hain, aur agar aapke paas clear roadmap nahi hoga, toh emotional trading aapka poora balance khali kar sakti hai. Ek solid trading plan aapko disciplined banata hai aur FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) se bachata hai.

🎯 **Step 1: Style aur Risk Profile Set Karein**

Pehle decide karein ke aap Scalper hain, Day Trader hain ya Swing Trader. Apne risk capacity ko samjhein—golden rule ye hai ke ek single trade par apne total account capital ka sirf 1% se 2% hi risk par lagayein.

📊 **Step 2: Entry aur Exit Rules Fix Karein**

Aap market mein enter kab karenge? Support/Resistance breakout par, Chart Patterns dekh kar, ya indicators par? Jab tak aapki strategy ke saare rules match na ho, tab tak buy ya sell button mat dabayein. Entry ke sath hi Stop Loss (SL) aur Take Profit (TP) pehle se decide hona chahiye.

📝 **Step 3: Trading Journal Banayein**

Apne har trade ka record rakhein—entry price, exit price, profit/loss aur sabse important: trade lene ka reason. Weekend par apni mistakes ko review karein taake aap next time unhe repeat na karein.

⚠️ **Important Disclaimer:** Crypto aur Forex market mein koi "Sure-shot Profit" ya magic formula nahi hota. Market hamesha unpredictable hoti hai, lekin ek disciplined trading plan aapke risk ko manage karta hai aur long-term success ke chances badha deta hai.
