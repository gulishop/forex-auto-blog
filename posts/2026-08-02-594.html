🕯️ Chart Read Karna Seekhna Hai? Ye 3 Candlestick Patterns Har Trader Ko Pata Hone Chahiye!

📅 02-08-2026

Trading charts dekh kar confuse ho jaate ho? Don't worry, har pro trader ki journey candlestick patterns ko samajhne se hi shuru hoti hai. Candlesticks market ki sentiment aur story batati hain—bas aapko unki language decode karni aani chahiye. Aayein 3 sabse powerful aur basic patterns ko samajhte hain:

🎯 **Doji (Market Indecision)**

Jab buyers aur sellers dono ka zor barabar ho, tab Doji banta hai. Isme candle ki opening aur closing price lagbhag same hoti hai. Ye signal deta hai ke current trend (bullish ya bearish) thak chuka hai aur market mein jaldi hi trend reversal ya pause aa sakta hai.

🔨 **Hammer & Inverted Hammer (Bullish Reversal)**

Agar market downtrend mein hai aur support level par ek Hammer candle bane, toh ye bullish reversal ka strong signal ho sakta hai. Ye dikhata hai ke sellers ne price ko neeche push kiya, lekin buyers ne aggressive entry le kar control apne haath mein le liya.

📈 **Engulfing Patterns (Strong Momentum)**

Bullish aur Bearish Engulfing patterns market ke strong momentum shift ko show karte hain. Bullish Engulfing mein ek badi green candle pichli choti red candle ko poori tarah cover ("engulf") kar leti hai. Ye batata hai ke buyers ab market par dominate kar rahe hain.

💡 **Pro Tip & Risk Disclaimer**

Humesha yaad rakhein, koi bhi candlestick pattern 100% profit ki guarantee nahi deta! In patterns ko humesha Support/Resistance levels aur indicators ke sath combine karke hi trade plan karein. Trading mein risk management sabse zaroori hai, isliye Stop Loss ka use zaroor karein.
