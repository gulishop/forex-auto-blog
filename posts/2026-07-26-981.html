Leverage: Trading Ka Superpower Ya Sab Se Bada Risk? 🚀

📅 26-07-2026

📊 **Leverage Asal Mein Kya Hai?** Simple words mein bolein toh leverage ek aisi facility hai jo aapka broker aapko deta hai, taake aap chote capital ke sath badi trades execute kar sakein. For example, agar aap 1:100 leverage use karte hain, toh aap apne $100 ke deposit se market mein $10,000 ki position control kar sakte hain. Yeh aap ke profit potential ko kafi barha deta hai.

⚠️ **Double-Edged Sword Se Hoshiyar Rahein:** Sunne mein yeh jitna exciting lagta hai, utna hi risky bhi hai. Leverage ek do-dhari talwar ki tarah kaam karta hai. Jitni speed se yeh aapke profits ko multiply karta hai, utni hi tezi se yeh aapka poora account wash bhi kar sakta hai. Beginners aksar greed mein aakar 1:500 ya 1:1000 leverage select kar lete hain, jo ke ek bohot badi galti hai.

💡 **Leverage Safe Tarike Se Kaise Use Karein?**

- **Always Start Low:** Agar aap beginner hain, toh 1:10 ya maximum 1:20 leverage se ziada mat jayein.

- **Never Skip Stop-Loss:** Market hamesha aap ke according nahi chalti. Stop-Loss lagana kabhi mat bhoolein taake aapka capital secure rahe.

- **Strict Risk Management:** Har trade par apne total account balance ka sirf 1% se 2% hi risk par lagayein, chahe leverage kitna bhi ho.

🎯 **Smart Trading Mindset:** Remember guys, Forex aur Crypto market mein koi "sure profit" ya shortcut nahi hota. Trading mein hamesha financial risk shamil hota hai, isliye professional traders leverage ko lalach ke liye nahi, balke smart position sizing ke liye use karte hain.
