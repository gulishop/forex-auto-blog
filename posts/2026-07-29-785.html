Demo Account Se Real Account Shift Kaise Karein? 3 Rules Jo Aapka Capital Bachayenge 🎯

📅 29-07-2026

💡 Demo account par toh har trade profit mein jati dikhti hai, lekin real account open karte hi panic kyun hone lagta hai? Reason bohot simple hai—Demo account mein fake funds ka sakoon hota hai, jabke real money ke saath emotions (Fear aur Greed) attach ho jate hain. Demo se real account tak ka safar sirf balance shift karne ka nahi, balki trading psychology ko master karne ka game hai.

⚠️ Rule 1: Hamesha Chote Deposit Se Start Karein! Demo par aapne shayad $10,000 ka virtual capital use kiya ho, lekin real account mein utna hi fund daalein jitna loss aap bina kisi emotional stress ke afford kar sakte hain. Shuruat mein micro-lots (0.01) se trade karein taake aap real market execution aur live spreads ko bina kisi bade risk ke samajh sakein.

📊 Rule 2: Process Par Focus Karein, Profit Par Nahi! Real trading mein aate hi raato-raat ameer banne ka mindset chor dein. Hamesha strict Risk-to-Reward ratio aur Stop Loss (SL) ka istemal karein. Demo account par jo discipline aapne build kiya tha, usko real money trading mein bhi strictly follow karein. Trading mein consistency sirf discipline se hi aati hai.

🎯 Rule 3: Trade Journal Banayein Aur Step-By-Step Badhein! Real account par apne har win aur loss trade ko record karein. Apni mistakes se seekhein aur jab aapka win-rate aur emotional control improve ho jaye, tabhi slowly apna position size badhayein. Trading ek marathon hai, koi short race nahi!
