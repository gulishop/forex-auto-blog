Support aur Resistance Sahi Tarike Se Kaise Identify Karein? Ye 3 Steps Apnayein 🎯

📅 29-07-2026

📈 Forex aur Crypto trading mein successful trades lene ke liye Support aur Resistance ko samajhna sabse pehla aur sabse important step hai. Simple words mein kahein toh Support ek "Floor" ki tarah hai jahan se price bounce karke upar jaati hai, aur Resistance ek "Ceiling" hai jahan se price reject ho kar niche aati hai. Agar aap in levels ko sahi se spot karna seekh gaye, toh aap market ke major reversal points ko pehle hi samajh sakte hain.

📊 **1. Multiple Touches Dhoondhein:** Single bounce par randomly line draw mat karein. Chart par aise price points dhoondhein jahan market ne kam se kam 2 ya 3 baar touch karke direction change ki ho. Jitne zayda touches honge, utna hi strong wo level mana jayega.

💡 **2. Single Line Nahi, "Zones" Draw Karein:** Naye traders sabse badi galti ye karte hain ke wo ek patli line draw kar dete hain. Price kabhi bhi exact number ko respect nahi karti. Hamesha ek price range ya "Zone" (box) draw karein taake aap fakeouts aur market noise se bach sakein.

🎯 **3. Higher Timeframes Ko Preference Dein:** 5-minute ya 15-minute chart par aapko har jagah S/R dikhega jo zyadatar weak hota hai. Sahi aur reliable key levels identify karne ke liye hamesha Daily ya 4-Hour (4H) timeframe se analysis start karein.

⚠️ **Pro Tip & Reality Check:** Yaad rakhein, market mein koi bhi technical tool 100% guaranteed profit nahi deta. S/R levels break bhi hote hain, isliye bina proper Risk Management aur Stop Loss ke kabhi koi trade execute mat karein.
