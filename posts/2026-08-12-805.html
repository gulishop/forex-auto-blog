Demo Account Se Real Trading Mein Switch Kaise Karein? Ye 4 Essential Rules Aapko Pata Hone Chahiye 📈

📅 12-08-2026

🚀 Demo account par profits dekh kar real market mein jump karna har beginner ka sapna hota hai, lekin real money ke sath market ki reality aur pressure double ho jate hain. Virtual money par profit kamana aasan lagta hai, par real trading mein aapki strategy ke sath-sath aapki trading psychology aur emotions ka asli test hota hai. Is safar ko safe banane ke liye ek sahi mindset ki zaroorat hai.

💰 **Small Capital Se Start Karein:** Real account kholte hi bada deposit karne ki galti kabhi mat karein. Shuruat mein utna hi capital invest karein jitna risk aap aasan se handle kar sakein. Small lot sizes se trading start karein taake real money ka emotional pressure aapke trading decisions ko kharab na kare.

🎯 **Strict Risk Management Rules Follow Karein:** Demo par hum aksar bina dar ke trades loss mein chalne dete hain, lekin real account mein hamesha Stop Loss (SL) aur Take Profit (TP) use karein. Ek single trade par apne account balance ka 1% se 2% se zyada risk na lein. Strategy wahi rakhein jo aapne demo par master ki hai.

⚠️ **Trading Journal Banayein Aur Discipline Rakhein:** Real trading mein fear (dar) aur greed (laalach) aap par haavi ho sakte hain. Har trade ki entry, exit aur reason ek notebook ya journal mein note karein. Apni galtiyon ko track karna hi aapko ek consistent aur disciplined trader banata hai. Market mein koi quick-rich scheme nahi hoti, sab kuch patience par depend karta hai.
