Top Candlestick Patterns Jo Har Trader Ko Pata Hone Chahiye 📈

📅 26-07-2026

💡 Trading chart par candles sirf red aur green boxes nahi hain, ye market ki actual psychology batati hain! Agar aap Forex ya Crypto market mein naye ho, toh candlestick patterns ko samajhna aapka pehla step hona chahiye. Sabse basic aur powerful hai **Hammer Pattern**. Ye aksar downtrend ke end par banta hai aur bullish reversal ka signal deta hai, matlab buyers market mein wapas enter ho rahe hain.

🚀 Dusra sabse popular pattern hai **Bullish & Bearish Engulfing**. Bullish Engulfing tab banta hai jab ek badi green candle pichli choti red candle ko poori tarah cover (engulf) kar leti hai. Ye strong buying momentum show karta hai. Iska opposite Bearish Engulfing hota hai, jo uptrend ke top par selling pressure aur potential drop ka warning sign hota hai.

🎯 Teesra crucial pattern hai **Doji**, jo market mein indecision (confusion) ko represent karta hai. Doji ka matlab hai ke buyers aur sellers dono ke beech fight chal rahi hai. Jab chart par Doji bane, toh samajh jao ke trend fast change ho sakta hai, lekin trade lene se pehle agli confirmation candle ka wait karna bohot zaroori hai.

⚠️ **Practical Tip & Risk Disclaimer:** Hamesha yaad rakho ke market mein 100% guarantee kisi cheez ki nahi hoti! Kisi bhi single pattern par blind trade mat lo. In patterns ko hamesha support/resistance levels aur proper risk management ke saath combine karke use karo. Pehle disciplined banen, phir profitable.
