Fundamental Vs Technical Analysis: Sahi Trading Strategy Kaise Chunein? 📊

📅 02-08-2026

📊 **Fundamental Analysis (FA) - Market Ki Real Story:** Trading mein success paane ke liye pehle market ka mood samajhna zaroori hai. Fundamental Analysis ka matlab hai kisi bhi asset ki core value check karna. Forex mein aap central bank policies, inflation rates aur economic news dekhte ho, jabke Crypto mein project ki utility aur adoption rate. Simple words mein, FA aapko ye batata hai ke koi coin ya currency intrinsic value ke hisab se strong hai ya weak, aur iski long-term direction kya ho sakti hai.

📈 **Technical Analysis (TA) - Charts Aur Price Action:** Agar FA market ka 'Kyun' hai, toh Technical Analysis market ka 'Kab' hai! TA mein hum past price history, candlestick patterns, support/resistance aur indicators (jaise RSI aur Moving Averages) ka use karte hain. Ye short-term traders aur intraday scalpers ke liye sabse zaroori tool hai kyunki ye aapko exact entry, exit aur stop-loss levels spot karne mein madad karta hai.

💡 **Pro Tip - Winning Combination Kaisa Banayein?** Boht se beginners puchte hain ke kaun sa method better hai? Best approach ye hai ke aap dono ko combine karein! Fundamentals se trend ki direction pakdein (kya buy/sell karna hai) aur Technicals se perfect entry point dhoondhein (kab trade mein enter hona hai). Yaad rakhein, market mein koi bhi strategy 100% profit guarantee nahi deti. Isliye bina proper risk management aur stop-loss ke kabhi trade mat karein.
