Crypto Market Ki Volatility Se Loss Kaise Bachayein? 3 Practical Tips 📈

📅 12-08-2026

📊 Crypto market mein ek hi din mein 10% se 20% ka pump ya dump dekhna bilkul normal baat hai. Is rapid price movement ko hi hum **Volatility** kehte hain. Naye traders aksar is speed se panic ho kar galat entries le lete hain, lekin sach yeh hai ke agar aap volatility ko samajh jayein, toh market ka darr hamesha ke liye khatam ho sakta hai.

🎯 **1. Stop Loss Ko Apna Sabse Bada Dost Banayein:** Volatile market mein bina Stop Loss (SL) ke trade karna matlab bina brakes ke car chalana hai. Hamesha har trade par apna risk pehle se fix karein—jaise apne total capital ka sirf 1% ya 2% risk par lagana. Yeh tarika aapke account ko kisi bhi sudden crash se safe rakhta hai.

⚠️ **2. FOMO Aur Emotional Trading Se Door Rahein:** Jab market mein badi green candles ban rahi hon, toh emotional ho kar top par buy mat karein. High volatility mein market hamesha pullback (dip) deti hai. Apni position sizing ko hamesha chota rakhein taake market ki sudden fluctuations aapki mental peace kharab na karein.

💡 **3. Smart Indicators Ka Use Karein:** Volatility ko measure karne ke liye **ATR (Average True Range)** aur **Bollinger Bands** jaise simple indicators ka istemal karein. Ye tools aapko batate hain ke market filhal kitni aggressive hai, jisse aapko sahi entry point aur realistic targets set karne mein madad milti hai.
