Crypto Volatility Kya Hai? Ye 3 Simple Rules Se Market Movement Ko Samjhein 📉

📅 30-07-2026

📊 Crypto market mein prices ka achanak 10% se 20% tak upar ya neeche jana bohot normal hai—isi tez price movement ko hum **Volatility** kehte hain. Naye traders aksar is sudden movement se darr jaate hain, lekin agar aap market dynamics ko ache se samjhein, toh volatility hi trading mein real profit opportunities create karti hai.

🛡️ **Rule 1: Risk Management Sabse Pehle:** Volatile market mein survive karne ka sabse bada secret hai strict Stop Loss (SL) ka use. Apni total capital ka sirf 1% se 2% hi ek single trade mein risk par lagayein. Market kitni bhi unpredictable ho, ek sahi jagah lagaya hua Stop Loss aapke account ko blow up hone se bachata hai.

📈 **Rule 2: Technical Indicators Aur News Ko Sahi Use Karein:** Price swings ko measure karne ke liye **ATR (Average True Range)** aur **Bollinger Bands** jaise tools ka use karein. Iske sath hi major crypto news aur market sentiment par bhi dhyan dein, kyunki ek badi news poori market ka trend moments mein badal sakti hai.

💡 **Rule 3: FOMO Aur Emotional Trading Se Bachein:** Jab market tezi se move kar rahi ho, toh FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) mein aakar top par buy mat karein. Hamesha apne trading plan par trust karein, patience rakhein aur emotional decisions ke bajaye sahi risk-to-reward ratio dekh kar hi entry lein.
