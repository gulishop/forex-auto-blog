Candlestick Patterns Jo Har Trader Ko Pata Hone Chahiye: Ye 3 High-Accuracy Signals Sikhein 🕯️📈

📅 06-08-2026

🚀 Chart dekhte waqt samajh nahi aata ke market upar jayegi ya neeche? Trading mein candlestick patterns aapke GPS ki tarah hain jo market ki agli move ka pehle se hint dete hain. Agar aap Forex ya Crypto market mein beginner hain, toh in teen powerful patterns ko samajhna aapki trading journey ka game-changer ban sakta hai.

💡 **1. Bullish Engulfing Pattern:** Jab ek chhoti red (bearish) candle ke baad ek badi green (bullish) candle banti hai jo pichli candle ko poori tarah cover kar le, toh isse Bullish Engulfing kehte hain. Ye strong signal hota hai ke sellers weak ho chuke hain aur buyers market ka control lene wale hain.

🎯 **2. Shooting Star (Bearish Signal):** Upar ki taraf lambi wick aur neeche ek chhoti body! Shooting Star tab banta hai jab buyers price ko upar push karte hain, lekin sellers zordar entry karke price ko neeche le aate hain. Strong resistance level par ye pattern ek potential downtrend ka hint deta hai.

📊 **3. Doji (Indecision Pattern):** Jab market ki opening aur closing price lagbhag same ho, tab Doji banta hai. Iska matlab hai buyers aur sellers ke beech tough fight chal rahi hai aur market confused hai. Doji dikhte hi fawran trade na karein, balke breakout confirmation ka wait karein.

⚠️ **Pro-Tip & Risk Alert:** Yaad rakhein, koi bhi candlestick pattern 100% guaranteed profit nahi deta. In patterns ko hamesha proper Risk Management, Stop Loss, aur Support/Resistance levels ke sath combine karke hi trade karein.
