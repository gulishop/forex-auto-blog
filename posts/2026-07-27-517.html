Trading Psychology: FOMO Aur Greed Se Bachne Ke 3 Practical Rules 🧠📈

📅 27-07-2026

💡 Trading sirf charts dekhna aur technical indicators apply karna nahi hai—80% success aapke mindset par depend karti hai! Jab market volatile hoti hai, toh FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) aur Greed jaisey emotions aapki saari strategy kharab kar dete hain. Uncontrolled emotions ki wajah se aksar traders impulsive decisions lete hain aur badle mein over-trading ya panic selling kar baithte hain.

🎯 Pre-Planned Strategy Follow Karein: Har trade open karne se pehle apna Entry Point, Take Profit (TP), aur Stop Loss (SL) fix karein. Jab aapka risk pehle se defined hota hai, toh market ke ups and downs mein panic hone ka chance bohot kam ho jata hai. Ek golden rule hamesha yaad rakhein—kabhi bhi ek single trade mein apne kul capital ka 1-2% se zyada risk mat lein.

📊 Trading Journal Banayein Aur Discipline Rakhein: Apne har trade ka record rakhein—aapne entry kyun li, TP hit hua ya SL, aur us time aapka emotional state kaisa tha. Jab bhi aapko lage ke aap loss recover karne ke liye (revenge trading) bina kisi setup ke trade kar rahe hain, toh turant screen off kar dein aur break lein. Self-control hi long-term success ki chabi hai.

⚠️ Profit Aur Loss Dono Trading Ka Hissa Hain: Market mein koi bhi strategy 100% guaranteed profit nahi deti, isliye blind risk kabhi mat lein. Consistent profit ke liye emotional discipline aur proper risk management sabse zaroori hai. Control your emotions before they control your account!
