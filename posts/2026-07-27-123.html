Stop Loss Aur Take Profit Sahi Jagah Kaise Set Karein? 3 Smart Rules 🎯

📅 27-07-2026

💡 Trading mein profit banana utna mushkil nahi hota, jitna apne balance ko protect karna. Stop Loss (SL) aur Take Profit (TP) aapke account ke safety airbags hain. Bina inke trade lagana matlab bina brakes ke gaadi chalana. Aaiye samajhte hain inka sahi use kaise karein!

🛡️ **Stop Loss Support/Resistance Par Lagayein:** Aksar beginner traders random dollars par SL set kar dete hain, jaise "$10 loss hua toh exit". Ye sabse badi galti hai! SL hamesha technical analysis par rakhein—jaise Key Support level ke thoda neeche ya Resistance ke thoda upar, taake market ki normal volatility aapko trade se bahar na kare.

🎯 **1:2 Risk-to-Reward Ratio Follow Karein:** Agar aap $10 ka risk (SL) le rahe hain, toh aapka TP kam se kam $20 hona chahiye. Is Rule ka fayda ye hai ke agar aapki 50% trades galat bhi ho jayein, tab bhi aap over-all profit mein rahenge. Trade lagane ke baad TP hit hone ka waite karein, jaldbazi mein exit na karein.

📊 **'Set and Forget' Mindset Apnayein:** Trade open karne ke baad apne SL aur TP ko baar baar shift na karein. Greed aur Fear trading ke sabse bade dushman hain. Ek golden rule hamesha yaad rakhein: Kisi bhi ek trade mein apne total capital ka sirf 1% se 2% hi risk par lagayein taake aapka account safe rahe.
