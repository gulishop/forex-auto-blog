Support aur Resistance Sahi Tarike Se Kaise Pehchanein? 3 Simple Rules 📈

📅 17-08-2026

🎯 Agar aap chart pe random buy/sell karke confuse ho rahe ho, toh ab ruk jao! Support aur Resistance (S&R) technical analysis ke wo foundational pillars hain jo market ka next move predict karne mein madad karte hain. Simply samjhein: **Support** ek "Floor" (zameen) hai jahan buyers active hote hain aur price bounce karti hai, jabki **Resistance** ek "Ceiling" (chhat) hai jahan sellers aate hain aur price reject hoti hai.

📊 **Rule 1: Multi-Touch Zones Dhoondein**

Chart par aise price points talash karein jahan market ne kam se kam 2 ya 3 baar touch karke reversal dikhaya ho. Hamesha yaad rakhein, Support ya Resistance koi ek single line nahi hoti, balki ek "Zone" ya area hota hai. Isliye single line draw karne ke bajaye hamesha rectangle tool se ek zone mark karein taake fakeout se bacha ja sake.

💡 **Rule 2: Higher Timeframe Par Bharosa Karein**

Agar aap sirf 5-minute chart dekh kar trade karoge, toh market noise aapka stop loss hit karwa degi. Pehle Daily (1D) ya 4-Hour (4H) chart par major key levels mark karein, aur phir 15-minute chart par ja kar confirmation ke sath entry plan karein. Higher timeframe ke levels hamesha zyada reliable hote hain.

⚠️ **Rule 3: Breakout aur Role Reversal Ko Samjhein**

Jab ek strong Resistance break hoti hai, toh wahi level aage chal kar naya Support ban jata hai (aur iska ulta bhi). Kabhi bhi breakout hote hi FOMO mein trade mat lein, hamesha price ke re-test aur confirmation candlestick (jaise pin bar ya engulfing candle) ka intezar karein. Trading mein patience hi asli edge hai.
