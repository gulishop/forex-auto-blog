Support Aur Resistance Sahi Tarike Se Kaise Identify Karein? Ye 3 Practical Rules Seekho 📊

📅 31-07-2026

🎯 Trading chart par Support aur Resistance bilkul ek kamre ki Zameen (Floor) aur Chhat (Ceiling) ki tarah hote hain. Support woh level hai jahan price neeche girte huye rukti hai aur bounce back karti hai (kyunki wahan buyers active hote hain). Jabki Resistance woh ceiling hai jahan price upar jaate huye reject hoti hai (jahan sellers control le lete hain). Agar aap in key levels ko pehchanna seekh gaye, toh aapki trade entry aur exit timings bohot improve ho sakti hain.

📊 In levels ko chart par spot karna bohot simple hai! Sabse pehle apne TradingView chart par higher timeframe (jaise 1H ya 4H) open karein. Phir dekho ke price kis specific level par kam se kam 2 se 3 baar touch karke reverse hui hai. Jis point par price baar baar ruk kar direction change kare, woh aapka Support ya Resistance zone ban jata hai. Ek baat hamesha yaad rakhein—S&R ek exact single line nahi, balki ek "Price Zone" hota hai.

💡 Naye traders aksar ek hi touch ko dekh kar jaldbazi mein trade le lete hain, jo sabse badi galti hai. Hamesha multi-touch test ka wait karein aur confirmation ke saath trade karein. Yaad rakhein, market mein koi bhi strategy 100% sure profit nahi deti. Risk hamesha rehta hai, isliye proper Stop Loss aur Risk Management ke bina kabhi trade na karein taake aapka capital safe rahe.
