Account Wash Hone Se Kaise Bachein? Stop Loss Aur Take Profit Ke 3 Golden Rules 🎯

📅 26-07-2026

⚠️ Forex aur Crypto market mein sabse badi galti hoti hai bina Stop Loss (SL) ke trade lagana. Think of SL as an airbag for your trading account—ye aapko ek bade crash ya account wash hone se bachata hai. Trade enter karne se pehle hamesha apna Risk-to-Reward ratio (minimum 1:2) fix karein. SL ko hamesha kisi strong Support/Resistance zone ya Market Structure ke bahar set karein, kisi random pips ke hisab se nahi.

🎯 Take Profit (TP) ka sahi use aapke greed (laalach) ko control karta hai. Bohot se beginner traders target set nahi karte aur market reverse hone par profit ko loss mein convert hote dekhte hain. Iska solution ye hai ke aap Next Key Resistance ya Support zone par apna TP lagayein. Smart move: Jab trade halfway hit kare, toh 50% partial profits lock karein aur SL ko breakeven (entry price) par move kar dein.

💡 Psychological discipline follow karna sabse zaroori hai. Trade lagane ke baad loss ke darr se SL ko baar baar peeche shift mat karein—ye ek bohot destructive habit hai. Ek strict rule banayein ke har single trade par apne total account balance ka sirf 1% se 2% hi risk karenge. Remember, professional traders risk control karke earn karte hain, tukke se nahi!
